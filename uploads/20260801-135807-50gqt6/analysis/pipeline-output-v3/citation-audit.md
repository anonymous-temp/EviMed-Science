## Unresolved items

1. **Henrikson et al. 2003 primary study (Annals of Internal Medicine)**:
   - This is the central study on nitroglycerin diagnostic value in the inpatient setting.
   - Not retrieved as independent full text or abstract. Referenced only through editorial [3] and systematic review [1].
   - The claim that nitroglycerin response had no diagnostic value in patients developing chest pain under medical supervision after admission is supported by the editorial and systematic review, but the primary study's methods, sample size, and precise effect estimates could not be independently verified.
   - **Action**: Obtain the primary article (Henrikson CA, et al. Ann Intern Med. 2003;139(10):832-7 or similar) for independent review.

2. **Prehospital delay quantitative data**:
   - While broad consensus exists that symptom-to-balloon time is an independent predictor of mortality in STEMI, specific hazard ratios and time-threshold data for Chinese populations were not retrieved at full-text level.
   - Several large-scale studies were returned as search results but not individually verified due to output truncation. The direction and clinical significance of the relationship are not in question, but precise effect estimates for specific delay intervals in the Chinese healthcare context should be verified from primary studies.

3. **Chinese guideline records (References 12, 13, 14)**:
   - Retrieved as structured guideline records through the EviMed guideline adapter.
   - The recommendations cited (e.g., "早期诊断，危险分层，正确分流，科学救治") come from structured summaries rather than independently verified guideline PDF full text.
   - Recommendation classes, levels of evidence, and exact original wording may differ from what is represented in the structured records.
   - **Action**: Cross-reference with the original published guideline documents.

4. **Suxiao Jiuxin Wan Chinese-language literature**:
   - The Cochrane review search ended in November 2005 [7]. A substantial body of Chinese-language literature published since then may exist but was not systematically captured by English-language database searches.
   - Chinese-language database searches returned zero results, suggesting the query structure or database access may not align with Chinese-language indexing conventions.
