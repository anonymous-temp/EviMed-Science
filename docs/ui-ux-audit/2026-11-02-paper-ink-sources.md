## Application boundaries

- R05/R06 (CDC, 2023) are historical product-method cases: page composition, shared primitives and brand intensity by touchpoint. They are not current EviMed evidence or a reason to replace its layout.
- R03 supports preserving expert users' whole-task visibility. DS22.6 already owns that decision; no new wizard policy is introduced.
- R07/R08 inform how professional judgment enters declarations, implementation and review contracts. The playbook is not a catalogue of page patterns. EviMed retains T1–T6, page-templates.json, its token package and its own components.
- R09 is an adjacent AI interaction case, not proof of clinical or research workflow correctness. Existing producer state and cancellation semantics remain authoritative.
- R01/R02 concern brand reuse and context-aware copy. Efficiency numbers and AI claims are not independently verified.
- R04 is a 2025 trend digest. It does not authorize glass, new colours, decorative metrics or low-contrast content in the workbench.
