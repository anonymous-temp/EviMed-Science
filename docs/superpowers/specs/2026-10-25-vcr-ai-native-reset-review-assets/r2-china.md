### A. Ten findings that should drive the reset

1. **No official Chinese text names digital twins, virtual patients or synthetic control arms.** In the six official texts I downloaded and counted (GCP 2026 and its Q&A, CDE 通告2026年第27号, 2025年第54号, 2026年第6号, NMPA 通告2020年第1号), 合成对照, 数字孪生 and 虚拟患者 occur 0 times; 外部对照 occurs 20 times, 模拟 82 times (all in the Bayesian guideline), 虚拟匹配对照 once. I found no other NMPA/CDE document on them. [OFFICIAL, own count] N18
2. **The regulator is cautious about external control.** 第27号 (signed 2026-03-25) s.2.4: external/historical control is "多数情况下不建议使用", acceptable only if the natural history is well established and the effect large. 第54号 (signed 2025-12-29): a single-arm trial can be confirmatory for regular approval of anticancer drugs only if RCT is infeasible, external-control data are clear, mechanism is clear and efficacy outstanding. NMPA 2020年第1号: parallel external control is "优于" historical; methods include propensity scores and "虚拟匹配对照", with sensitivity and quantitative-bias analysis. [OFFICIAL] N9, N10, N16
3. **通告2026年第6号 (Bayesian external-information borrowing, 试行, signed 2026-01-20) is the best official template for hybrid-control and virtual-trial outputs.** Its named use case is rare-disease studies that "借用历史外部对照信息"; it requires an applicability assessment with simulation, evidence of comparability, false-positive control so borrowing "不会主导试验结果", false-positive simulations of "例如至少100000次", supplementary simulation if design or data leave the pre-specified range, an example simulation report (Appendix 2), and CDE communication at pre-IND, protocol change and pre-NDA. [OFFICIAL] N16
4. **GCP 2026 (公告2026年第50号, signed 2026-05-21, published 2026-06-08, in force 2026-09-01) is silent on AI, simulation, virtual patients, external control and RWD.** What it does say: Art.13 (new technology must meet ethics, science and law), Art.14(一) (the ethics committee reviews "招募试验参与者的方式和信息"), Art.21(一) (the PI must have the time and ability to enrol enough eligible 试验参与者 within the contract period), Chapter 5 and Art.53 (validated computerized systems). 通告2026年第44号 (2026-09-22) makes the sponsor finally responsible for system validation and entitles it to the supplier's validation documents. 受试者 is now 试验参与者. [OFFICIAL; 第44号 via vendor summary] N14, N10
5. **The media line "NMPA and three departments encouraged virtual patients" has no title or number.** FDA's M15 notice was in the Federal Register on 2026-06-03; the four-department GCP followed on 2026-06-08 (five days later) and contains no such clause, only Art.13 and a Q&A sentence supporting "新技术、新方法". Do not cite it as regulatory support. [MEDIA; inference] N1, N15
6. **Chinese "AI recruitment" is mostly operations with an algorithmic front end.** 零氪's 厚普医药: 400 full-time recruiters, ~1,000 sites, a patient pool, "算法匹配叠加人工复核" [MEDIA 2024-12-20]. Matching-engine claims (水滴/翼帆 ">92%", 太美 "6x") come with no protocol, sample or data source [VENDOR]. "Digital twin" means omics-level indication selection for pharma (哲源, ~RMB 200M A2, 2026-08-05) or a hospital intention (齐鲁, RMB 2.625M, reported 2026-09-28; no tender or award found). N8, N11, N15
7. **The CDE trial platform is the source 患者找试验 cannot do without, and it is hard to read.** About 36k records on 2026-07-30, 5,942 recruiting and 4,232 not yet recruiting [VENDOR]; no NCT field; no API, bulk download or robots.txt; anti-bot (curl got a JS challenge, headless Chromium HTTP 400) [own check]; a quote-based commercial API exists (摩熵数科). It is not an ICTRP data provider [OFFICIAL: WHO lists 18 registries]. N5, N7, N17
8. **ClinicalTrials.gov alone misses most Chinese industry trials**: about 64% of 38,558 China industry interventional trials are not on it; CDE-ChiCTR overlap is under 200 [VENDOR, TrialForge 2026-07]. N7
9. **Hospital data rules put the duty on the hospital.** 国卫规划发〔2026〕6号 (dated 2026-02 by secondary sources): entrusting processing does not shift responsibility; written contract and impact assessment before entrusting; cloud services must be security-assessed; derived data take the source's class; AI use needs a risk assessment; "原始数据不出域，数据可用不可见" is encouraged. [OFFICIAL text via mirror] N13
10. **No Chinese real-EHR evaluation of trial matching with published accuracy was found.** CHIP-CTC labels criteria sentences only (44 classes; best baseline macro-F1 70.9, human 78.0); CHIP 2025 Task 3 uses 51 criteria and 51 case reports for FHIR code generation; the one Chinese-institution EHR paper used English criteria, GPT-4 and 41 patients (accuracy 0.88). [PAPER] N12

### E. Data constraints (Q3, product level, not legal advice)

- Hospital measures (N13): classes core/important/general; derived data re-classified from the source; important data stored in China; export needs assessment and separate consent. Large-model training is not addressed.
- NHC and four departments' "AI+医疗卫生" opinion (reported 2025-12-02, https://www.ncsti.gov.cn/kjdt/kjrd/202512/t20251202_230756.html): aims by 2027 at "高质量数据集和可信数据空间", "建立大模型应用评测验证" and a data-security protection system; no clause on clinical trials. 2026 enforcement climate [MEDIA, https://www.163.com/dy/article/KVI5GJNM0519DDQ2.html]: a CAC-led personal-information campaign in the health sector from 2026-04 and an NHC-led notice of 2026-06 listing "严守医疗数据安全"; a survey quoted there says 84.86% of hospitals rely only on confidentiality agreements with third parties.
- PIPL and cross-border [VENDOR summary of statutes, 2026-09-14]: medical-health data are sensitive personal information needing separate consent; a CAC security assessment is required once sensitive data of 10,000 individuals have been exported in a year. A model hosted inside China does not itself trigger the export rules [inference]. HGR rules exclude clinical and imaging data but cover genome data (N6).

### N3. Bayesian external-information borrowing guidance (Q3)
- 《药物临床试验中应用贝叶斯外部信息借用方法的指导原则（征求意见稿）》: draft posted by a trade site 2025-08-19 (https://m.xfdyb.com/show-2348.html, attributes to CDE; CDE's own draft date and comment deadline not on that page). Scope per the draft: pivotal trials where a Bayesian method is the primary analysis; exploratory/sensitivity/subgroup Bayesian analyses "may refer to it". Content per the draft summary: applicability assessment (scientific rationale, exchangeability, source-data quality, feasibility, interpretability), borrowing methods (power prior, mixture prior named), simulation requirements + example simulation report (Appendix 2), type-I-error ("错判概率") to be kept "in a reasonable range", borrowing must "不会主导试验结果", false-positive simulations ">= 100000" iterations (as reported), three regulator-communication scenarios (pre-trial, pre-NDA, protocol change). The phrase "外部对照" itself does NOT appear in the draft per that summary; closest passage is historical-control borrowing in rare disease.
- FINAL (试行): 《药物临床试验中应用贝叶斯外部信息借用方法的指导原则（试行）》 reported released ~2026-01-21 by 医药魔方/ByDrug https://bydrug.pharmcube.com/news/detail/d32c614ceb1cecb0174a6a323f464cef (CDE drafted under NMPA deployment, NMPA approved, "自发布之日起施行"). ### N6. HGR / PIPL / data export facts (Q3, product-team level, not legal advice) - from EClinCloud 2026-09-14 summary; vendor commentary, primary texts to be cross-checked
- HGR: 《人类遗传资源管理条例》(国务院令717号, 2019-05-28; revised by 令777号, 2024-03-10) + 《实施细则》(科技部令21号, issued 2023-05-26, effective 2023-07-01). NHC has administered the regime since 2024-05-01. Scope: HGR info = gene/genome data; Implementing Rules Art.2 "excludes clinical data, imaging data, protein data and metabolic data". International collaboration needs approval (Art.22 of Regulations); registration-directed trials with no HGR material export need filing only (Implementing Rules Art.32). Outbound HGR information needs prior report + back-up (Art.36); security review for e.g. exome/genome sequencing of >500 people (Art.37). Fines 500k-5M CNY.
  => Implication: a pure clinical-record workflow (diagnoses, labs, imaging reports, medications) is outside HGR by definition; anything with genome/exome data is not.
- PIPL (2021-11-01): medical-health data = sensitive personal information (Art.28), separate consent (Art.29); cross-border: CAC security assessment / certification / standard contract (Art.38-40), notice + separate consent (Art.39), PIA (Art.55). CAC 《促进和规范数据跨境流动规定》 (Order 16, effective 2024-03-22) thresholds: security assessment for CIIO exports, important data exports, or cumulative since Jan 1 of the year >= 1,000,000 individuals' non-sensitive PI or >= 10,000 individuals' sensitive PI; standard contract/certification for 100k to <1M non-sensitive or <10k sensitive; exempt <100k non-sensitive/year (Arts. 3-5, assess exemptions first). Process clocks: security assessment (Order 11, 2022-09-01) 5+7 working days then 45 working days; standard contract (Order 13, 2023-06-01) PIA first, filing within 10 working days.
  => For a product that sends Chinese patient text to a model hosted INSIDE China (DeepSeek via control plane), the cross-border chapter is not triggered by the model call itself; PIPL sensitive-PI consent/purpose limitation still apply; hospital-side rules (医疗卫生机构 data security, 医院 may not let records leave the intranet) are usually the real blocker. ### N12. Chinese benchmarks for criteria parsing / matching (Q4)

## Implications for the EviMed reset

Evidence is cited by note (N#) with the URL and date in that note; the recommendations are my inference from it, not findings. Anything legal needs counsel.

### Scope
- **Keep the four tasks, but name them in the regulator's words in Chinese copy**: 外部对照 / 历史对照 / 外部信息借用 (not 合成对照), 模拟研究 / 操作特征 (not "虚拟试验" as a regulatory claim), 试验参与者 in text for trial teams. Evidence: 合成对照, 数字孪生 and 虚拟患者 occur 0 times in the six official texts I counted; 外部对照 occurs 20 times and 模拟 82 times (N18).
- **Treat 数字孪生 / 虚拟患者 as a market label, not a regulatory category.** Buyers who use it mean omics-level twins for indication selection (哲源, 2026-08-05) or hospital-side virtual population modelling (齐鲁 intention, RMB 2.625M, original notice unverified) (N11, N15). Do not cite the media line that four departments "encourage virtual patients": it has no title or number, and the GCP published 2026-06-08 contains no such clause (N1, N14, N15).
- **患者匹配 is the one task with an investigator-facing user.** The GCP asks the PI to have the time and ability to enrol enough eligible 试验参与者 (Art.21(一)) and has the ethics committee review recruitment methods and information (Art.14(一)) (N14). Ending at a candidate list for the investigator's review, with per-criterion evidence and no outreach, fits both. No vendor I read shows per-criterion evidence or a published validation (N8, N12), so that is the gap this scope can fill, not another recruitment funnel.
- **Sequence by data access** [inference]: patient→trials from a user-supplied, identifier-free summary plus public registries needs no hospital data pipeline; trial→patients on a cohort table needs patient-level data and so brings in the hospital-measures regime (N13).
- **A virtual cohort built from patient records is derived data.** Under the hospital measures derived data are re-classified from the source (Art.8); whether a fully synthetic cohort escapes that is an open question for counsel (N13).

### Removing recruitment
- **The Chinese market evidence supports removing the operations layer.** Recruitment revenue stories rest on people and networks: 400 full-time recruiters and ~1,000 sites (厚普/零氪), a hospital network, patient community and follow-up (泽雅), a recruitment centre (京东健康) (N8). A referral, CRM or follow-up funnel would move EviMed into that business, with ethics-reviewed recruitment materials (GCP Art.14(一)) and patient-contact exposure under sensitive-personal-information rules (N14, N6, N13). I found no evidence that an analysis-only product needs that layer.
- **Keep, as analysis with no patient contact** [inference]: (1) criteria parsing into structured per-criterion checks; (2) feasibility counts on a user-supplied cohort and "which criterion removes the most candidates"; (3) a registry-based competing-trial and status view. Market reason: vendors already offer sponsors and CROs site selection and enrolment-speed insight (零氪 "PRO+" high-volume centres; 太美 centre screening, self-reported) (N8), and enrolment capacity is a condition the PI and site must meet (Art.21(一)). Caveat: I found no Chinese price point or willingness-to-pay for standalone AI feasibility analysis; the only budget signal is the 齐鲁 intention for virtual population modelling, which is not feasibility (N15).
- **Remove**: outreach, referral tracking, follow-up tasks, patient community.

### Registry coverage 患者找试验 needs
- **Minimum set: CDE platform + ChiCTR + ClinicalTrials.gov.** ICTRP is no substitute (it does not include the CDE platform). ClinicalTrials.gov alone would miss about 64% of TrialForge's 38,558 China industry interventional trials, and CDE and ChiCTR barely overlap (<200), since CDE takes only NMPA-authorised trials while ChiCTR also takes investigator-initiated ones (N5, N7) [VENDOR counts].
- **Access is the hard part.** No API, bulk download or robots.txt; a JS anti-bot layer blocks curl and headless Chromium; terms of use unknown. Do not plan around evasion (the open-source collector I read itself says not to bypass access controls) (N17). Options: a licence (摩熵数科 sells a quote-based "中国临床试验" API), permission from CDE, or user-session-assisted retrieval. Budget a human check of one official multi-centre record before building any site-level feature: I could not settle whether the official page has a per-site table with PIs or untruncated criteria (N17).
- **Identity and dedup.** CDE has no NCT field and one protocol can have several CTR numbers, so the candidate list needs a protocol-level identity step (N5, N7).
- **Status semantics.** Sponsors have 30 days to update after an event; the CDE pool on 2026-07-30 was 5,942 recruiting plus 4,232 not yet recruiting [VENDOR]; status is trial-level and site-level recruiting is unverified. Show the snapshot date next to registry status (whether an as-of date counts as back-office provenance under the owner's 2026-10-10 ruling is the owner's call) (N5, N15).
- **Eligibility text can be truncated on mirrors.** When a criterion's text is missing or cut, decline that criterion with a reason instead of guessing, in line with the owner's 2026-09-29 decision (N17).
- **Not researched**: investigator-initiated studies registered only in the national medical-research registration system.

### Data-requirement disclosure
- **Evidence.** Vendors rarely state their data sources (太美, 水滴) (N8). A hospital is the responsible party and must approve the processor in writing, run an impact assessment, use a security-assessed cloud, and treat derived data at the source's class (hospital measures Arts.8, 16, 17, 22, 27) (N13). A sponsor must validate its systems and may demand the supplier's validation documents (GCP Art.53; 通告2026年第44号) (N14, N10).
- **Recommendation** [inference]: give every task a short data sheet, in the conversation and in the docs: required inputs and minimum fields (say "no identifiers needed" where true, e.g. patient→trials); what is sent to which model or service and where it is processed; retention; who the responsible party is; what is derived, and at what class. For 外部对照 and 虚拟试验 add the source list, selection process, covariates, missingness and comparability evidence, which is what 第6号 and the RWD guideline (2021年第27号) tell the sponsor to document (N16, N9).
- **Warn when a cohort carries genome or sequencing data**: HGR rules exclude clinical and imaging data but not genome data (N6) [VENDOR summary].
- **Open item for ops and counsel**: the hospital measures require a cloud service that has passed the cloud-computing security assessment (Art.16(二)); I could not verify that status for any model provider, nor whether it applies to model API calls (N13).

### Regulatory framing for 合成对照 and 虚拟试验 outputs
- **Frame them as decision support and material for 沟通交流 with CDE, not as a replacement for a control arm.** 第27号 s.2.4: external/historical control is "多数情况下不建议使用"; 第54号 allows single-arm confirmation only for anticancer drugs and only when "外部对照数据充分可靠"; NMPA 2020年第1号 prefers parallel external control and asks for sensitivity and quantitative-bias analysis (N9, N10, N16).
- **Hybrid control is the named use case.** 第6号 defines external information borrowing by the example of rare-disease studies that "借用历史外部对照信息". The most defensible 合成对照 framing is "borrowed external information with a discount and a false-positive check", not "virtual arm" (N16).
- **Shape the report like the guideline's Appendix 2 (simulation report)**: source-selection record, comparability and exchangeability assessment, estimand and covariates, simulation plan, operating characteristics, false-positive simulations at high replication (the guideline's example is "至少100000次"), supplementary simulation when design or data leave the pre-specified range, plus the sensitivity and quantitative-bias analysis of RWE 2020. Deterministic engines compute; the model explains (N16).
- **RWE 2020 is the only official text of the six that mentions AI/ML** (as methods needing more expertise), and it requires that "数据元素和转化数据的算法均应保证其正确": the nearest official hook for NLP/LLM evidence extraction, so show per-criterion source text and extraction checks (N18).
- **If outputs enter a submission, the sponsor owns validation** (GCP Art.53; 第44号), so plan a validation dossier for the deterministic engines and keep the language model out of regulated numbers (N10, N14).
- **M15**: if CDE finalises its proposal, M15 applies to studies starting 6 months after the final announcement; I did not read M15's text, so I make no claim about its credibility framework (N9).
- **Wording**: 试验参与者 in text for trial teams (GCP Art.54(一)); 患者 is fine for patient-facing text (N14).
