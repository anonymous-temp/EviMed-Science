## Drug selection: source version and regulatory status

The completed NVAF DOAC comparison (run `run_ce2834372b2fb74dc8ad01c0bb31c411`) correctly withheld a ranking without an institutional rubric and described differences between the RE-LY trial abstract and a later US label. Its summary nevertheless combined the original article's 1.11% annual dabigatran rate with the later label's 1.72% warfarin rate and 0.65 hazard ratio as one result. The [original RE-LY article](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa0905561) reports 1.11%, 1.69% and 0.66; the [PRADAXA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=ba74e3cd-b06f-4145-b284-5fd6b84ff3c9) reports 1.12%, 1.72% and 0.65. These source versions must not be merged into a single row without labeling their provenance.

The report used a 2025 apixaban label to discuss andexanet and contrasted it with the rivaroxaban label, while describing the latter as current. The [FDA's withdrawn accelerated approvals](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/accelerated-approval-program/withdrawn-non-malignant-hematological-neurological-and-other-disorder-indications-accelerated) records Andexxa's withdrawal on 2025-12-23, and its [2026 apixaban labeling approval](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/2026/202155Orig1s042%2C%20220073Orig1s001ltr.pdf) removes that language. This is US regulatory evidence; it does not establish Chinese approval or hospital availability. The report separately called local status unverified, but its current-availability implication remains unreliable. The report also stated a renal threshold without its lower boundary and drew an overbroad safety contrast from separate warfarin-controlled trials. The run's own record noted two review suggestions not addressed. A current-label and numerical-source check on the candidate model is still required.

## Review: follow-up windows and interpretation

The production ASPREE review (run `run_cea33793efdde9adecdfbe1c81888038`) delivered its report, Word copy and evidence matrix. The main 4.7-year cardiovascular and major haemorrhage rates match the [2018 trial](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1805819). Its reply places 67 versus 62 fatal haemorrhages in the same paragraph as those trial-period findings without identifying that these counts are from the combined in-trial and post-trial window in the [extended-follow-up table](https://academic.oup.com/view-large/539368177). It then infers that the excess bleeding consists of nonfatal events, which those counts do not establish. A further statement treats a lack of detected meta-regression interaction as ruling out a risk-dependent benefit. Those claims require revision; a quoted source or a clean automated receipt does not establish that a number's time window and inference were preserved. A candidate replay and independent review remain pending.

## Meta analysis: synthetic arithmetic and model interpretation

The production browser run used three explicitly synthetic 2x2 tables: 12/100 vs 20/100, 18/120 vs 25/120, and 8/80 vs 12/80. Its stated DerSimonian–Laird RR 0.668, 95% CI 0.458–0.974, Q 0.173, I² 0% and tau² 0 match an independent inverse-variance calculation. It reported the synthetic boundary and did not invent references or a GRADE rating. It justified random effects in part by unequal sample sizes and control rates; those alone do not demonstrate heterogeneity of effects. Its leave-one-out discussion emphasized confidence intervals crossing one; lower power after removing a study alone does not show effect instability. The updated skill and evaluation criteria address these interpretations, but no candidate replay has established behavioral improvement. Retrieval-based systematic-review flow was not exercised by this synthetic test.

## Candidate-host limitation

A local throwaway-account run of `diagnostic-interpretation` used the candidate control plane and pinned DSH 0.2.1-rc.5. The macOS host could run `sandbox-exec`, but the staged hosted profile selected `/usr/local/lib/evimed/evimed-landlock-run`, a Linux-image path unavailable on the host. The run was canceled at its 15-minute timeout after four tool calls; execution and agent evidence were incomplete and the throwaway account was deleted. Swapping to a host sandbox without demonstrating the project's workspace-read and gateway isolation would not establish equivalent runtime protection, so no model-quality pass is claimed. Evidence remains under the local candidate results directory; a safely isolated Linux candidate runtime is still needed.

The DSH document-preview package calls the upstream `MarkdownText` primitive; [its architecture note](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness/blob/master/.agents/notes/implemented/architecture/2026-08-06-web-markdown-incremental-ast-renderer.md) identifies the GFM/micromark parse path. A future fix should exercise the native document preview and conversation renderer with Chinese punctuation, valid internationalized domains, explicit Markdown links, code spans and streaming boundaries. Rewriting source bytes or patching only the outer SaaS Markdown viewer would not establish a correct fix.
