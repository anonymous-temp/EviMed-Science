# Research Tools cards and copy

The Research Tools catalogue follows the owner's reference: a responsive grid
of cards, grouped by the existing categories. At desktop widths it has three
columns; at medium widths two; at narrow widths one. Each card keeps its icon,
title, category, full description, estimated duration and optional availability
or allowance. The whole card opens the bound conversation; a connector link is
an independent focusable action. No new form or alternate Agent is introduced.

The twelve retained workflows keep their capability IDs and contracts. Their
display titles and single-sentence descriptions use familiar research terms and
avoid promising that an optional method or source always succeeds. The former
clinical-evidence title is displayed as `文献综述`; the existing capability remains
the workflow's identity. Former tool names are searchable for three months,
with stored historical titles and session bindings unchanged. The generic
statistical skill is labelled `统计分析方法参考`, distinct from the research
workflow's `统计分析` entry.

## References for wording and scope

- [Cochrane software overview](https://www.cochrane.org/learn/courses-and-resources/software-training): conventional names for systematic review and meta-analysis; software stages and certainty assessment are distinct.
- [Covidence protocol guide](https://www.covidence.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/A_practical_guide_Protocol_Development_for_Systematic_Reviews.pdf): literature import, screening and data extraction terminology.
- [OpenVigil](https://openvigil.sourceforge.net/): drug-safety signal terminology and limitations of spontaneous reports.
- [Galaxy](https://galaxyproject.org/): upload, analysis and reproducibility as recognizable data-analysis concepts.
- [UCI Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/17/breast+cancer+wisconsin+diagnostic): public data used for the real upload scenario. The copy used here is the repository's frozen fixture; source class 0 means malignant and 1 benign.

These references inform the wording and test input; they do not establish that
our workflows equal those products or that a scientific result is validated.
Actual platform runs, their outputs, citations and independent numerical checks
must be recorded separately. No deployment is included in this change.
