## What the numbers say

- **Primary arm, 76 to 78 weeks (4 arms):** the model predicts less worsening than the trials saw, in every arm (mean bias -1.51 points, from -0.58 for IDENTITY to
  -2.69 for the bapineuzumab carriers); all four observed values lie inside the predictive interval. **24 weeks (4 arms, all on a cholinesterase inhibitor):** the model
  predicts more worsening than the trials saw, in every arm (mean bias +0.74); all four are inside the interval. Eight arms, so each mean carries a wide uncertainty of
  its own; the two signs are consistent within a horizon, which is what the data of this set show and nothing more.
- **The predictive interval is wide**: 8.7 to 8.8 points at 76 to 78 weeks and 3.0 to 3.3 at 24 weeks. It carries the parameter uncertainty of the posterior draws and the
  study-to-study variation, which is what a new trial's arm mean really varies by in this model. That the observed values fall inside it, 8 of 8, is a weak test: the
  eight z-scores lie between -1.22 and +1.22, and a narrower interval would have missed some. The bias, not the coverage, is the figure to read.
- **No background arm** moves every prediction up by about 0.3 points (toward the observed at 76 to 78 weeks, mean bias -1.23; away from it at 24 weeks, +0.97): the
  background is a small effect here, since three months of stable dose have already spent most of it. Neither choice was tuned and the primary stands as frozen.
- **What the descriptions show** (not fitted, not corrected): the model's typical-study baseline is 22.5 to 23.5 for the 78-week arms (reported 21.7 to 23.9) but 28.3
  to 30.2 for the 24-week arms (reported 24.8 to 25.9): the model's mapping from MMSE to a baseline score gives higher baselines at the lower MMSE the short trials
  enrol (entry 12 to 24 and 12 to 22). The change is counted from each person's own baseline, so the bias above is not that difference, but a starting score that is 3 to 4
  points off is a premise of its own for these arms. The model's own dropout leaves 62 to 64% in the study at week 76 to 78 against 54 to 75% reported, and 81 to 82% at
  week 24 against 89%.
- **The second structure (Conrado 2014)** predicts less spread between trials (its interval is subject-level only: the study-level terms were not used) and a mean
  that differs from the primary's by 1.63 points on average at 76 to 78 weeks and 0.91 at 24 weeks. At 76 to 78 weeks its mean bias is +0.12 (mean absolute 0.50), closer than
  the primary's -1.51; at 24 weeks it is +1.65, further. Its coverage (3 of 4 and 0 of 4) is not comparable with the primary's, because its interval is narrow by omission.
  The two structures disagree by more than the primary's bias at 78 weeks, which is the model-form uncertainty that more simulated trials do not reduce. Nothing is averaged;
  no weight is validated. This is a spread between two published structures fitted to the same CAMD database, not between independent data.

## What is not claimed

Validation of any drug effect (only placebo arms); a narrow bias interval from eight arms; a verified transcription of Conrado's parameters (the paper's tables were not
open: the values are the authors' NONMEM listing as transcribed by an open library); that none of the eight arms is in the CAMD database the primary model was fitted to
(see the protocol).

## Limits met

- IDENTITY's MMSE range, APOE4 shares and background therapy are assumptions of the trial table; the idalopirdine and EPOCH carrier splits borrow bapineuzumab 301's.
- The visit schedules are simplified; EPOCH's and IDENTITY's early stops are carried by the MMRM's missing-at-random reading, not by imposing the trials' dropout.
