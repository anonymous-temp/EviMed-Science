# Evidence map

One row per work or record located and used. `Reading level` records how far the work was actually read — a record-only row supports the existence of the work and nothing about its design, sample or results. `Channel` names the source through which the record was located. `Axis` is the research-topic axis the work informs: subject, method, comparator, or absence (that is, evidence used to bound a claim that something was not found).

Each work was reached through one of several routes, named in the `Channel` column: the PubMed literature index; Europe PMC, whose index spans the full text of open-access articles; the OpenAlex scholarly catalogue; publisher DOI records reached through Crossref; clinical practice guideline documents; trial registries; and open web search leads, which were followed to their primary records before any use.
