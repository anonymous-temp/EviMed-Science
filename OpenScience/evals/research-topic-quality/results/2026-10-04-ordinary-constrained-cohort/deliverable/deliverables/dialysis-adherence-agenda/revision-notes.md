# Revision notes

Notes for the record on what was changed in this deliverable and why. Nothing in
this file is part of the report's argument.

## Traceability review of the draft (citation, number and figure checks)

**Citations.** Every DOI in the reference list was resolved against a public
registry and every PMID was fetched back and compared. All resolved; titles,
authors and years matched the entries as printed. No unresolvable identifier was
found. Titles that could not be retrieved beyond their index entry (two
editorials and one conference abstract, and the works listed at record level in
the field map) are cited at that level only.

**Numbers.** Each statistic in the report was traced to the source that carries
it. Where a figure comes from a preserved full text, the source string was
compared with the report string; where it comes from an abstract or an abstract
book entry, the abstract was re-read before the figure was accepted. Counts made
inside this run — search scope counts, reading counts, the register-entry count —
were recomputed from the files that hold the records.

**Repairs made in this pass.**

1. The sentence on measurement instruments in the field map attributed a count of
   three to the most recent integrative review. The review names six references
   that combined subjective adherence questions with objective indicators or
   medical records. The sentence was corrected to the review's own statement.
2. One field-map row, and five further rows in the epidemiology, outcome and
   transport sections, described the content of works that were reached at index
   level only. Those rows now state what they are — records located by title, an
   editorial, a conference abstract whose text could not be retrieved — and no
   row claims a result for a work whose text was not read.
3. Two statements that shortened sessions outnumber missed ones "by a factor of
   four to five" presented a ratio as if a source had published it. No source
   states that ratio. The wording now gives the two source figures side by side
   and labels the comparison as this report's own arithmetic over them, with the
   range corrected to roughly four- to six-fold.

**Left as they stand.** Where a source's own numbers were reproduced, they were
reproduced as published, including two figures that differ slightly from the
arithmetic one would derive from the same paper's other numbers; the report
attributes each to the source rather than adjusting it. Recurring decimal
approximations differ by one decimal place between some abstracts and their
summary sentences; the report reports the abstract's form.

## Figure and code check

The report contains no figures, no generated images and no derived tables
produced by analysis code, so there is nothing to reconcile against a generating
script. All tables are assembled from the cited works and from the record counts
described above.

## Language pass

Applied as the last step, after the evidence work was finished. The pass changed
sentence rhythm, paragraph openings and transitions only, and left every
quotation, number, confidence interval, citation index, section heading and
reference entry byte-identical. A mechanical comparison of the document before
and after the pass confirms no number and no citation index changed and that no
part of the reference list moved.
