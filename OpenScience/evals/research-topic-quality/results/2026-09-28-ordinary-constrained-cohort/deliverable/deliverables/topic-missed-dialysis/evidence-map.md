## Notes on this map and its evidence levels

One row per work consulted. `Used for` names the sentence or question that depends on the row; a row that could not name one was removed. `Full text` is `yes` only where the complete open-access article was preserved alongside this package and its Methods or Limitations were read directly; the count is recomputed from the preserved articles, not self-reported.

Channels: `pubmed`, `europe-pmc`, `openalex`, `crossref`, `guideline`, `trial-registry`.

- Two rows are **conference abstracts**, not full papers: the USRDS count-band analysis (https://doi.org/10.1053/j.ajkd.2012.02.181) and the machine-learning fluid-admission intervention (https://doi.org/10.1093/ndt/gfae069.824). Both are used only for the existence of the approach and its reported headline figures, and are labelled as abstracts where cited.
- The KDOQI row carries no registry identifier. Its URL is the published guideline text, which was read directly; the recommendation quoted in §3.1 is verbatim from that page.
- Every other row is used at abstract level, and no row is used to assert a study design, effect size or limitation that its abstract does not state.
- The `openalex` row for the ESRD-AQ record carries an OpenAlex work URL because no DOI was returned with it; it is used only for the instrument's reported psychometric properties.
