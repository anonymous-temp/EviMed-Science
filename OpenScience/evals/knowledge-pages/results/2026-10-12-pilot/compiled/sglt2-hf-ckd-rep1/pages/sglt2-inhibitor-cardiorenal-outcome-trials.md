# SGLT2 inhibitor cardiorenal outcome trials compared

## Population and ejection fraction

## Primary outcome definitions

- The heart failure trials used a composite of cardiovascular death with worsening heart failure or hospitalization for heart failure as the primary outcome, while the kidney trials used composites of kidney disease progression with death from renal or cardiovascular causes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"The primary outcome was a composite of worsening heart failure (hospitalization or an urgent visit resulting in intravenous therapy for heart failure) or cardiovascular death."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"The primary outcome was a composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for worsening heart failure."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"The primary outcome was a composite of a sustained decline in the estimated GFR of at least 50%, end-stage kidney disease, or death from renal or cardiovascular causes."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_9babb063478831150659a9e2e6b0e443","quote":"The primary outcome was a composite of progression of kidney disease (defined as end-stage kidney disease, a sustained decrease in eGFR to \u003c10 ml per minute per 1.73 m2, a sustained decrease in eGFR of ≥40% from baseline, or death from renal causes) or death from cardiovascular causes."} --> <!-- evimed-statement:{"kind":"synthesized","confidence":"high"} -->
