# CREDENCE

## Design

- CREDENCE was a double-blind, randomized trial that assigned patients with type 2 diabetes and albuminuric chronic kidney disease to canagliflozin 100 mg daily or placebo, all with an estimated GFR of 30 to <90 ml per minute per 1.73 m2 of body-surface area and albuminuria (ratio of albumin [mg] to creatinine [g], >300 to 5000) and treated with renin-angiotensin system blockade. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_57ce472296b677f0d08707b04c50a8f0","quote":"In this double-blind, randomized trial, we assigned patients with type 2 diabetes and albuminuric chronic kidney disease to receive canagliflozin, an oral SGLT2 inhibitor, at a dose of 100 mg daily or placebo. All the patients had an estimated glomerular filtration rate (GFR) of 30 to \u003c90 ml per minute per 1.73 m2 of body-surface area and albuminuria (ratio of albumin [mg] to creatinine [g], \u003e300 to 5000) and were treated with renin-angiotensin system blockade."} -->
- The primary outcome of CREDENCE was a composite of end-stage kidney disease (dialysis, transplantation, or a sustained estimated GFR of <15 ml per minute per 1.73 m2), a doubling of the serum creatinine level, or death from renal or cardiovascular causes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_57ce472296b677f0d08707b04c50a8f0","quote":"The primary outcome was a composite of end-stage kidney disease (dialysis, transplantation, or a sustained estimated GFR of \u003c15 ml per minute per 1.73 m2), a doubling of the serum creatinine level, or death from renal or cardiovascular causes."} -->

## Results

- In CREDENCE, the relative risk of the renal-specific composite of end-stage kidney disease, a doubling of the creatinine level, or death from renal causes was lower by 34%. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_57ce472296b677f0d08707b04c50a8f0","quote":"The relative risk of the renal-specific composite of end-stage kidney disease, a doubling of the creatinine level, or death from renal causes was lower by 34% (hazard ratio, 0.66; 95% CI, 0.53 to 0.81; P\u003c0.001), and the relative risk of end-stage kidney disease was lower by 32% (hazard ratio, 0.68; 95% CI, 0.54 to 0.86; P = 0.002)."} -->
- In CREDENCE, 4401 patients had undergone randomization at the time of the interim analysis, with a median follow-up of 2.62 years. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_57ce472296b677f0d08707b04c50a8f0","quote":"At that time, 4401 patients had undergone randomization, with a median follow-up of 2.62 years."} -->
