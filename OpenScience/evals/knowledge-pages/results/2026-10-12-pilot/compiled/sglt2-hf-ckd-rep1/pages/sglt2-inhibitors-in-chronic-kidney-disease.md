# SGLT2 inhibitors in chronic kidney disease

## Dapagliflozin in chronic kidney disease

- In patients with chronic kidney disease, dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily reduced the composite of a sustained decline in estimated GFR of at least 50%, end-stage kidney disease, or death from renal or cardiovascular causes compared with placebo over a median of 2.4 years. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"Over a median of 2.4 years, a primary outcome event occurred in 197 of 2152 participants (9.2%) in the dapagliflozin group and 312 of 2152 participants (14.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.61; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.51 to 0.72; P\u003c0.001; number needed to treat to prevent one primary outcome event, 19 [95% CI, 15 to 27])."} -->
- In DAPA-CKD, the effects of dapagliflozin were similar in participants with type 2 diabetes and in those without type 2 diabetes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"The effects of dapagliflozin were similar in participants with type 2 diabetes and in those without type 2 diabetes."} -->
- In DAPA-CKD, the independent data monitoring committee recommended stopping the trial because of efficacy. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"The independent data monitoring committee recommended stopping the trial because of efficacy."} -->

## Empagliflozin in chronic kidney disease

- In patients with chronic kidney disease at risk for disease progression, empagliflozin 10 mg once daily reduced the composite of progression of kidney disease or death from cardiovascular causes compared with placebo during a median of 2.0 years of follow-up. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_9babb063478831150659a9e2e6b0e443","quote":"During a median of 2.0 years of follow-up, progression of kidney disease or death from cardiovascular causes occurred in 432 of 3304 patients (13.1%) in the empagliflozin group and in 558 of 3305 patients (16.9%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.72; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.64 to 0.82; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- In EMPA-KIDNEY, results were consistent among patients with or without diabetes and across subgroups defined according to eGFR ranges. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_9babb063478831150659a9e2e6b0e443","quote":"Results were consistent among patients with or without diabetes and across subgroups defined according to eGFR ranges."} -->
- In EMPA-KIDNEY, the rate of hospitalization from any cause was lower in the empagliflozin group than in the placebo group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_9babb063478831150659a9e2e6b0e443","quote":"The rate of hospitalization from any cause was lower in the empagliflozin group than in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.86; 95% CI, 0.78 to 0.95; P = 0.003)"} -->
- In EMPA-KIDNEY, there were no significant between-group differences with respect to the composite outcome of hospitalization for heart failure or death from cardiovascular causes or death from any cause. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_9babb063478831150659a9e2e6b0e443","quote":"there were no significant between-group differences with respect to the composite outcome of hospitalization for heart failure or death from cardiovascular causes (which occurred in 4.0% in the empagliflozin group and 4.6% in the placebo group) or death from any cause (in 4.5% and 5.1%, respectively)"} -->

## Canagliflozin in type 2 diabetes and nephropathy

- In patients with type 2 diabetes and albuminuric chronic kidney disease, canagliflozin 100 mg daily reduced the composite of end-stage kidney disease, a doubling of the serum creatinine level, or death from renal or cardiovascular causes compared with placebo at a median follow-up of 2.62 years. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_57ce472296b677f0d08707b04c50a8f0","quote":"The relative risk of the primary outcome was 30% lower in the canagliflozin group than in the placebo group, with event rates of 43.2 and 61.2 per 1000 patient-years, respectively (hazard ratio, 0.70; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.59 to 0.82; P = 0.00001)."} -->
- In CREDENCE, the canagliflozin group also had a lower risk of cardiovascular death, myocardial infarction, or stroke and of hospitalization for heart failure. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_57ce472296b677f0d08707b04c50a8f0","quote":"The canagliflozin group also had a lower risk of cardiovascular death, myocardial infarction, or stroke (hazard ratio, 0.80; 95% CI, 0.67 to 0.95; P = 0.01) and hospitalization for heart failure (hazard ratio, 0.61; 95% CI, 0.47 to 0.80; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- In CREDENCE, there were no significant differences in rates of amputation or fracture. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_57ce472296b677f0d08707b04c50a8f0","quote":"There were no significant differences in rates of amputation or fracture."} -->
- The CREDENCE trial was stopped early after a planned interim analysis on the recommendation of the data and safety monitoring committee. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_57ce472296b677f0d08707b04c50a8f0","quote":"The trial was stopped early after a planned interim analysis on the recommendation of the data and safety monitoring committee."} -->
