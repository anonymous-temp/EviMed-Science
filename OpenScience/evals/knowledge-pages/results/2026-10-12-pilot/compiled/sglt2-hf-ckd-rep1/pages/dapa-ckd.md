# DAPA-CKD

## Design

- DAPA-CKD randomly assigned 4304 participants with an estimated GFR of 25 to 75 ml per minute per 1.73 m2 of body-surface area and a urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio of 200 to 5000 to dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily or placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"We randomly assigned 4304 participants with an estimated glomerular filtration rate (GFR) of 25 to 75 ml per minute per 1.73 m2 of body-surface area and a urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio (with albumin measured in milligrams and creatinine measured in grams) of 200 to 5000 to receive dapagliflozin (10 mg once daily) or placebo."} -->
- The primary outcome of DAPA-CKD was a composite of a sustained decline in the estimated GFR of at least 50%, end-stage kidney disease, or death from renal or cardiovascular causes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"The primary outcome was a composite of a sustained decline in the estimated GFR of at least 50%, end-stage kidney disease, or death from renal or cardiovascular causes."} -->

## Results

- In DAPA-CKD, the hazard ratio for the composite of a sustained decline in the estimated GFR of at least 50%, end-stage kidney disease, or death from renal causes was 0.56. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"The hazard ratio for the composite of a sustained decline in the estimated GFR of at least 50%, end-stage kidney disease, or death from renal causes was 0.56 (95% CI, 0.45 to 0.68; P\u003c0.001), and the hazard ratio for the composite of death from cardiovascular causes or hospitalization for heart failure was 0.71 (95% CI, 0.55 to 0.92; P = 0.009)."} -->
- In DAPA-CKD, death occurred in 101 participants (4.7%) in the dapagliflozin group and 146 participants (6.8%) in the placebo group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"Death occurred in 101 participants (4.7%) in the dapagliflozin group and 146 participants (6.8%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.69; 95% CI, 0.53 to 0.88; P = 0.004)."} -->
- In DAPA-CKD, the known safety profile of dapagliflozin was confirmed. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"The known safety profile of dapagliflozin was confirmed."} -->
