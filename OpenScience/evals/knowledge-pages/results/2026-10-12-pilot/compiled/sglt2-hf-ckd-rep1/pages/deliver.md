# DELIVER

## Design

- DELIVER randomly assigned 6263 patients with heart failure and a left ventricular ejection fraction of more than 40% to dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily or matching placebo, in addition to usual therapy. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"We randomly assigned 6263 patients with heart failure and a left ventricular ejection fraction of more than 40% to receive dapagliflozin (at a dose of 10 mg once daily) or matching placebo, in addition to usual therapy."} -->
- The primary outcome of DELIVER was a composite of worsening heart failure (an unplanned hospitalization for heart failure or an urgent visit for heart failure) or cardiovascular death, assessed in a time-to-event analysis. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"The primary outcome was a composite of worsening heart failure (which was defined as either an unplanned hospitalization for heart failure or an urgent visit for heart failure) or cardiovascular death, as assessed in a time-to-event analysis."} -->

## Results

- In DELIVER, worsening heart failure occurred in 368 patients (11.8%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 455 patients (14.5%) in the placebo group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"Worsening heart failure occurred in 368 patients (11.8%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 455 patients (14.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.79; 95% CI, 0.69 to 0.91)"} -->
- In DELIVER, cardiovascular death occurred in 231 patients (7.4%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 261 patients (8.3%) in the placebo group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"cardiovascular death occurred in 231 patients (7.4%) and 261 patients (8.3%), respectively (hazard ratio, 0.88; 95% CI, 0.74 to 1.05)."} -->
- In DELIVER, total events and symptom burden were lower in the dapagliflozin group than in the placebo group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"Total events and symptom burden were lower in the dapagliflozin group than in the placebo group."} -->
