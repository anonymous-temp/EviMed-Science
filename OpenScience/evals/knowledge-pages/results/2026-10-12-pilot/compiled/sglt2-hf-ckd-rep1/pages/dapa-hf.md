# DAPA-HF

## Design

- DAPA-HF was a phase 3, placebo-controlled trial that randomly assigned 4744 patients with New York Heart Association class II, III, or IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of 40% or less to dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily or placebo, in addition to recommended therapy. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"In this phase 3, placebo-controlled trial, we randomly assigned 4744 patients with New York Heart Association class II, III, or IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of 40% or less to receive either dapagliflozin (at a dose of 10 mg once daily) or placebo, in addition to recommended therapy."} -->
- The primary outcome of DAPA-HF was a composite of worsening heart failure (hospitalization or an urgent visit resulting in intravenous therapy for heart failure) or cardiovascular death. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"The primary outcome was a composite of worsening heart failure (hospitalization or an urgent visit resulting in intravenous therapy for heart failure) or cardiovascular death."} -->

## Results

- In DAPA-HF, a first worsening heart failure event occurred in 237 patients (10.0%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 326 patients (13.7%) in the placebo group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"A first worsening heart failure event occurred in 237 patients (10.0%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 326 patients (13.7%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.70; 95% CI, 0.59 to 0.83)."} -->
- In DAPA-HF, death from cardiovascular causes occurred in 227 patients (9.6%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 273 patients (11.5%) in the placebo group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"Death from cardiovascular causes occurred in 227 patients (9.6%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 273 patients (11.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.82; 95% CI, 0.69 to 0.98)"} -->
- In DAPA-HF, 276 patients (11.6%) in the dapagliflozin group and 329 patients (13.9%) in the placebo group died from any cause. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"276 patients (11.6%) and 329 patients (13.9%), respectively, died from any cause (hazard ratio, 0.83; 95% CI, 0.71 to 0.97)."} -->
