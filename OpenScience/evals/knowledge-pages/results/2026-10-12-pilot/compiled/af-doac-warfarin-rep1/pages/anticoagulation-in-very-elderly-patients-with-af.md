# Anticoagulation in very elderly patients with atrial fibrillation

## The problem of bleeding risk

- Implementation of appropriate oral anticoagulant treatment for the prevention of stroke in very elderly patients with atrial fibrillation is challenging because of concerns regarding bleeding. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_0e9d75e95b4aa172897de8e3f4333228","quote":"Implementation of appropriate oral anticoagulant treatment for the prevention of stroke in very elderly patients with atrial fibrillation is challenging because of concerns regarding bleeding."} -->

## Low-dose edoxaban versus placebo

- In very elderly Japanese patients (≥80 years of age) with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation who were not appropriate candidates for standard doses of oral anticoagulants, a once-daily 15-mg dose of edoxaban was superior to placebo in preventing stroke or systemic embolism and did not result in a significantly higher incidence of major bleeding than placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_0e9d75e95b4aa172897de8e3f4333228","quote":"In very elderly Japanese patients with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation who were not appropriate candidates for standard doses of oral anticoagulants, a once-daily 15-mg dose of edoxaban was superior to placebo in preventing stroke or systemic embolism and did not result in a significantly higher incidence of major bleeding than placebo."} -->
- The annualized rate of stroke or systemic embolism was 2.3% with edoxaban 15 mg and 6.7% with placebo (hazard ratio, 0.34; 95% CI, 0.19 to 0.61; P<0.001), while the annualized rate of major bleeding was 3.3% with edoxaban and 1.8% with placebo (hazard ratio, 1.87; 95% CI, 0.90 to 3.89; P=0.09). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_0e9d75e95b4aa172897de8e3f4333228","quote":"The annualized rate of stroke or systemic embolism was 2.3% in the edoxaban group and 6.7% in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.34; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.19 to 0.61; P\u003c0.001), and the annualized rate of major bleeding was 3.3% in the edoxaban group and 1.8% in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 1.87; 95% CI, 0.90 to 3.89; P = 0.09)."} -->
- There were substantially more events of gastrointestinal bleeding in the edoxaban group than in the placebo group, and no substantial between-group difference in death from any cause (9.9% with edoxaban and 10.2% with placebo; hazard ratio, 0.97; 95% CI, 0.69 to 1.36). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_0e9d75e95b4aa172897de8e3f4333228","quote":"There were substantially more events of gastrointestinal bleeding in the edoxaban group than in the placebo group.
