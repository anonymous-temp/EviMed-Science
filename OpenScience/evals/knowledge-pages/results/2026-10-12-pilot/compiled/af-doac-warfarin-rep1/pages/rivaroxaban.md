# Rivaroxaban

## Efficacy and safety versus warfarin in atrial fibrillation

- In patients with atrial fibrillation, rivaroxaban was noninferior to warfarin for the prevention of stroke or systemic embolism. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8ff4bad2e9d926335b4de77ad1e41630","quote":"In patients with atrial fibrillation, rivaroxaban was noninferior to warfarin for the prevention of stroke or systemic embolism."} -->
- There was no significant between-group difference in the risk of major bleeding, although intracranial and fatal bleeding occurred less frequently in the rivaroxaban group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8ff4bad2e9d926335b4de77ad1e41630","quote":"There was no significant between-group difference in the risk of major bleeding, although intracranial and fatal bleeding occurred less frequently in the rivaroxaban group."} -->
- In the primary analysis, the primary end point occurred in 188 patients in the rivaroxaban group (1.7% per year) and in 241 in the warfarin group (2.2% per year) (hazard ratio 0.79; 95% CI, 0.66 to 0.96; P<0.001 for noninferiority). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8ff4bad2e9d926335b4de77ad1e41630","quote":"In the primary analysis, the primary end point occurred in 188 patients in the rivaroxaban group (1.7% per year) and in 241 in the warfarin group (2.2% per year) (hazard ratio in the rivaroxaban group, 0.79; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.66 to 0.96; P\u003c0.001 for noninferiority)."} -->
- Major and nonmajor clinically relevant bleeding occurred in 1475 patients in the rivaroxaban group (14.9% per year) and in 1449 in the warfarin group (14.5% per year) (hazard ratio, 1.03; 95% CI, 0.96 to 1.11; P=0.44). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8ff4bad2e9d926335b4de77ad1e41630","quote":"Major and nonmajor clinically relevant bleeding occurred in 1475 patients in the rivaroxaban group (14.9% per year) and in 1449 in the warfarin group (14.5% per year) (hazard ratio, 1.03; 95% CI, 0.96 to 1.11; P=0.44), with significant reductions in intracranial hemorrhage (0.5% vs. 0.7%, P=0.02) and fatal bleeding (0.2% vs. 0.5%, P=0.003) in the rivaroxaban group."} -->
- The trial was a double-blind trial that randomly assigned 14,264 patients with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation at increased risk for stroke to rivaroxaban 20 mg daily or dose-adjusted warfarin. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8ff4bad2e9d926335b4de77ad1e41630","quote":"In a double-blind trial, we randomly assigned 14,264 patients with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation who were at increased risk for stroke to receive either rivaroxaban (at a daily dose of 20 mg) or dose-adjusted warfarin."} -->

## Japan-specific dose in Japanese patients (J-ROCKET AF)

- In Japanese patients with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation at increased risk for stroke, a Japan-specific rivaroxaban dose of 15 mg once daily was noninferior to warfarin dose-adjusted according to Japanese guidelines for the principal safety outcome of major and non-major clinically relevant bleeding. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81808a7c4c3bf0e34bac1c720768f46d","quote":"Patients (n=1,280) with non-valvular AF at increased risk for stroke were randomized to receive 15 mg once-daily rivaroxaban or warfarin dose-adjusted according to Japanese guidelines. The primary objective was to determine non-inferiority of rivaroxaban against warfarin for the principal safety outcome of major and non-major clinically relevant bleeding, in the on-treatment safety population."} -->
- In J-ROCKET AF, the rate of the principal safety outcome was 18.04% per year in rivaroxaban-treated patients and 16.42% per year in warfarin-treated patients (hazard ratio 1.11; 95% CI 0.87-1.42; P<0.001 for non-inferiority). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81808a7c4c3bf0e34bac1c720768f46d","quote":"Non-inferiority of rivaroxaban to warfarin was confirmed; the rate of the principal safety outcome was 18.04% per year in rivaroxaban-treated patients and 16.42% per year in warfarin-treated patients (hazard ratio [HR] 1.11; 95% confidence interval 0.87-1.42; P\u003c0.001 [non-inferiority])."} -->
- In J-ROCKET AF, intracranial hemorrhage rates were 0.8% with rivaroxaban and 1.6% with warfarin, with a strong trend for a reduction in the rate of stroke/systemic embolism with rivaroxaban versus warfarin (HR, 0.49; P=0.050). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81808a7c4c3bf0e34bac1c720768f46d","quote":"Intracranial hemorrhage rates were 0.8% with rivaroxaban and 1.6% with warfarin. There was a strong trend for a reduction in the rate of stroke/systemic embolism with rivaroxaban vs. warfarin (HR, 0.49; P=0.050)."} -->
