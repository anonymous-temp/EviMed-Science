# Dabigatran

## Efficacy and safety versus warfarin in atrial fibrillation

- In patients with atrial fibrillation, dabigatran at a dose of 110 mg twice daily was associated with rates of stroke and systemic embolism similar to those with warfarin, and with lower rates of major hemorrhage. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8a669b066c4cb002e5224ef48c9a8550","quote":"In patients with atrial fibrillation, dabigatran given at a dose of 110 mg was associated with rates of stroke and systemic embolism that were similar to those associated with warfarin, as well as lower rates of major hemorrhage."} -->
- In patients with atrial fibrillation, dabigatran at a dose of 150 mg twice daily, as compared with warfarin, was associated with lower rates of stroke and systemic embolism but similar rates of major hemorrhage. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8a669b066c4cb002e5224ef48c9a8550","quote":"Dabigatran administered at a dose of 150 mg, as compared with warfarin, was associated with lower rates of stroke and systemic embolism but similar rates of major hemorrhage."} -->
- In the trial, rates of the primary outcome of stroke or systemic embolism were 1.69% per year with warfarin, 1.53% per year with dabigatran 110 mg, and 1.11% per year with dabigatran 150 mg. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8a669b066c4cb002e5224ef48c9a8550","quote":"Rates of the primary outcome were 1.69% per year in the warfarin group, as compared with 1.53% per year in the group that received 110 mg of dabigatran (relative risk with dabigatran, 0.91; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.74 to 1.11; P\u003c0.001 for noninferiority) and 1.11% per year in the group that received 150 mg of dabigatran (relative risk, 0.66; 95% CI, 0.53 to 0.82; P\u003c0.001 for superiority)."} -->
- The rate of major bleeding was 3.36% per year with warfarin, 2.71% per year with dabigatran 110 mg (P=0.003), and 3.11% per year with dabigatran 150 mg (P=0.31). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8a669b066c4cb002e5224ef48c9a8550","quote":"The rate of major bleeding was 3.36% per year in the warfarin group, as compared with 2.71% per year in the group receiving 110 mg of dabigatran (P=0.003) and 3.11% per year in the group receiving 150 mg of dabigatran (P=0.31)."} -->
- The rate of hemorrhagic stroke was 0.38% per year with warfarin, 0.12% per year with dabigatran 110 mg, and 0.10% per year with dabigatran 150 mg. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8a669b066c4cb002e5224ef48c9a8550","quote":"The rate of hemorrhagic stroke was 0.38% per year in the warfarin group, as compared with 0.12% per year with 110 mg of dabigatran (P\u003c0.001) and 0.10% per year with 150 mg of dabigatran (P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- The trial was a noninferiority trial that randomly assigned 18,113 patients with atrial fibrillation and a risk of stroke to fixed doses of dabigatran 110 mg or 150 mg twice daily, or to unblinded adjusted-dose warfarin, with a median follow-up of 2.0 years. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8a669b066c4cb002e5224ef48c9a8550","quote":"In this noninferiority trial, we randomly assigned 18,113 patients who had atrial fibrillation and a risk of stroke to receive, in a blinded fashion, fixed doses of dabigatran--110 mg or 150 mg twice daily--or, in an unblinded fashion, adjusted-dose warfarin. The median duration of the follow-up period was 2.0 years."} -->
