# ASPREE trial

## Design and population

- ASPREE enrolled community-dwelling men and women in Australia and the United States who were 70 years of age or older (or 65 years of age or older among blacks and Hispanics in the United States) and did not have cardiovascular disease, dementia, or disability. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e9c7fb4c1ac80a88d29e42eb4e27608e","quote":"From 2010 through 2014, we enrolled community-dwelling men and women in Australia and the United States who were 70 years of age or older (or ≥65 years of age among blacks and Hispanics in the United States) and did not have cardiovascular disease, dementia, or disability."} -->
- ASPREE participants were randomly assigned to receive 100 mg of enteric-coated aspirin or placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e9c7fb4c1ac80a88d29e42eb4e27608e","quote":"Participants were randomly assigned to receive 100 mg of enteric-coated aspirin or placebo."} -->
- The primary end point of ASPREE was a composite of death, dementia, or persistent physical disability, and secondary end points included major hemorrhage and cardiovascular disease. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e9c7fb4c1ac80a88d29e42eb4e27608e","quote":"The primary end point was a composite of death, dementia, or persistent physical disability; results for this end point are reported in another article in the Journal. Secondary end points included major hemorrhage and cardiovascular disease (defined as fatal coronary heart disease, nonfatal myocardial infarction, fatal or nonfatal stroke, or hospitalization for heart failure)."} -->
- Of the 19,114 persons enrolled in ASPREE, 9525 were assigned to receive aspirin and 9589 to receive placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e9c7fb4c1ac80a88d29e42eb4e27608e","quote":"Of the 19,114 persons who were enrolled in the trial, 9525 were assigned to receive aspirin and 9589 to receive placebo."} -->

## Results

- In ASPREE, after a median of 4.7 years of follow-up, the rate of cardiovascular disease was 10.7 events per 1000 person-years in the aspirin group and 11.3 in the placebo group (HR 0.95; 95% CI 0.83-1.08). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e9c7fb4c1ac80a88d29e42eb4e27608e","quote":"After a median of 4.7 years of follow-up, the rate of cardiovascular disease was 10.7 events per 1000 person-years in the aspirin group and 11.3 events per 1000 person-years in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.95; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.83 to 1.08)."} -->
- In ASPREE, the rate of major hemorrhage was 8.6 events per 1000 person-years with aspirin and 6.2 with placebo (HR 1.38; 95% CI 1.18-1.62; P<0.001). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e9c7fb4c1ac80a88d29e42eb4e27608e","quote":"The rate of major hemorrhage was 8.6 events per 1000 person-years and 6.2 events per 1000 person-years, respectively (hazard ratio, 1.38; 95% CI, 1.18 to 1.62; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- In ASPREE, a total of 1052 deaths occurred during a median of 4.7 years of follow-up, and the risk of death from any cause was 12.7 events per 1000 person-years in the aspirin group and 11.1 in the placebo group (HR 1.14; 95% CI 1.01-1.29). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_400696c0380783b1753ade39d010ccba","quote":"A total of 1052 deaths occurred during a median of 4.7 years of follow-up. The risk of death from any cause was 12.7 events per 1000 person-years in the aspirin group and 11.1 events per 1000 person-years in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 1.14; 95% confidence interval [CI], 1.01 to 1.29)."} -->
- In ASPREE, cancer was the major contributor to the higher mortality in the aspirin group, accounting for 1.6 excess deaths per 1000 person-years, and cancer-related death occurred in 3.1% of the aspirin group versus 2.3% of the placebo group (HR 1.31; 95% CI 1.10-1.56). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_400696c0380783b1753ade39d010ccba","quote":"Cancer was the major contributor to the higher mortality in the aspirin group, accounting for 1.6 excess deaths per 1000 person-years. Cancer-related death occurred in 3.1% of the participants in the aspirin group and in 2.3% of those in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 1.31; 95% CI, 1.10 to 1.56)."} -->
