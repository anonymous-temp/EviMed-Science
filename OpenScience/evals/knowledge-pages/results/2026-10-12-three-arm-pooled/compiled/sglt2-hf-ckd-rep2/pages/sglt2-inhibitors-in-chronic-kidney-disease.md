# SGLT2 inhibitors in chronic kidney disease

## Dapagliflozin in chronic kidney disease

- In patients with chronic kidney disease, with or without type 2 diabetes, dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily reduced the composite of a sustained decline in estimated GFR of at least 50%, end-stage kidney disease, or death from renal or cardiovascular causes compared with placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"We randomly assigned 4304 participants with an estimated glomerular filtration rate (GFR) of 25 to 75 ml per minute per 1.73 m2 of body-surface area and a urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio (with albumin measured in milligrams and creatinine measured in grams) of 200 to 5000 to receive dapagliflozin (10 mg once daily) or placebo. The primary outcome was a composite of a sustained decline in the estimated GFR of at least 50%, end-stage kidney disease, or death from renal or cardiovascular causes."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"a primary outcome event occurred in 197 of 2152 participants (9.2%) in the dapagliflozin group and 312 of 2152 participants (14.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.61; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.51 to 0.72; P\u003c0.001; number needed to treat to prevent one primary outcome event, 19 [95% CI, 15 to 27])"} -->
- In that trial the hazard ratio for the composite of death from cardiovascular causes or hospitalization for heart failure was 0.71, and death occurred in 4.7% of the dapagliflozin group and 6.8% of the placebo group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"the hazard ratio for the composite of death from cardiovascular causes or hospitalization for heart failure was 0.71 (95% CI, 0.55 to 0.92; P = 0.009). Death occurred in 101 participants (4.7%) in the dapagliflozin group and 146 participants (6.8%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.69; 95% CI, 0.53 to 0.88; P = 0.004)."} -->
- The trial was stopped early on the recommendation of the independent data monitoring committee because of efficacy. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"The independent data monitoring committee recommended stopping the trial because of efficacy."} -->

## Empagliflozin in chronic kidney disease

- In that trial the rate of hospitalization from any cause was lower with empagliflozin, but there were no significant between-group differences for the composite of hospitalization for heart failure or death from cardiovascular causes, or for death from any cause. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_9babb063478831150659a9e2e6b0e443","quote":"The rate of hospitalization from any cause was lower in the empagliflozin group than in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.86; 95% CI, 0.78 to 0.95; P = 0.003), but there were no significant between-group differences with respect to the composite outcome of hospitalization for heart failure or death from cardiovascular causes (which occurred in 4.0% in the empagliflozin group and 4.6% in the placebo group) or death from any cause (in 4.5% and 5.1%, respectively)."} -->

## Canagliflozin in type 2 diabetes and nephropathy

- In that trial the canagliflozin group also had a lower risk of cardiovascular death, myocardial infarction, or stroke, and of hospitalization for heart failure, with no significant differences in rates of amputation or fracture. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_57ce472296b677f0d08707b04c50a8f0","quote":"The canagliflozin group also had a lower risk of cardiovascular death, myocardial infarction, or stroke (hazard ratio, 0.80; 95% CI, 0.67 to 0.95; P = 0.01) and hospitalization for heart failure (hazard ratio, 0.61; 95% CI, 0.47 to 0.80; P\u003c0.001). There were no significant differences in rates of amputation or fracture."} -->
- The trial was stopped early after a planned interim analysis on the recommendation of the data and safety monitoring committee. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_57ce472296b677f0d08707b04c50a8f0","quote":"The trial was stopped early after a planned interim analysis on the recommendation of the data and safety monitoring committee."} -->

## Consistency across diabetes status

- In the dapagliflozin chronic kidney disease trial, the effects were similar in participants with type 2 diabetes and in those without type 2 diabetes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"The effects of dapagliflozin were similar in participants with type 2 diabetes and in those without type 2 diabetes."} -->
- In the empagliflozin chronic kidney disease trial, results were consistent among patients with or without diabetes and across subgroups defined according to eGFR ranges. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_9babb063478831150659a9e2e6b0e443","quote":"Results were consistent among patients with or without diabetes and across subgroups defined according to eGFR ranges."} -->
