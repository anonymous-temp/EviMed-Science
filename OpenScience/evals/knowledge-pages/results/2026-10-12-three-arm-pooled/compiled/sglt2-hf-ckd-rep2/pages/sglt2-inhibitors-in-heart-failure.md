# SGLT2 inhibitors in heart failure

## Effect in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction

- In patients with heart failure and an ejection fraction of 40% or less, dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily added to recommended therapy reduced the composite of worsening heart failure or cardiovascular death compared with placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"we randomly assigned 4744 patients with New York Heart Association class II, III, or IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of 40% or less to receive either dapagliflozin (at a dose of 10 mg once daily) or placebo, in addition to recommended therapy. The primary outcome was a composite of worsening heart failure (hospitalization or an urgent visit resulting in intravenous therapy for heart failure) or cardiovascular death."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"the primary outcome occurred in 386 of 2373 patients (16.3%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 502 of 2371 patients (21.2%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.74; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.65 to 0.85; P\u003c0.001)"} -->
- In the same trial, death from cardiovascular causes occurred in 9.6% of the dapagliflozin group and 11.5% of the placebo group, and death from any cause in 11.6% and 13.9%, respectively. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"Death from cardiovascular causes occurred in 227 patients (9.6%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 273 patients (11.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.82; 95% CI, 0.69 to 0.98); 276 patients (11.6%) and 329 patients (13.9%), respectively, died from any cause (hazard ratio, 0.83; 95% CI, 0.71 to 0.97)."} -->
- In patients with class II, III, or IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of 40% or less, empagliflozin 10 mg once daily reduced the composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for worsening heart failure compared with placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"we randomly assigned 3730 patients with class II, III, or IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of 40% or less to receive empagliflozin (10 mg once daily) or placebo, in addition to recommended therapy. The primary outcome was a composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for worsening heart failure."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"a primary outcome event occurred in 361 of 1863 patients (19.4%) in the empagliflozin group and in 462 of 1867 patients (24.7%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio for cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure, 0.75; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.65 to 0.86; P\u003c0.001)"} -->
- In that trial the total number of hospitalizations for heart failure was lower with empagliflozin than with placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"The total number of hospitalizations for heart failure was lower in the empagliflozin group than in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.70; 95% CI, 0.58 to 0.85; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- In the two trials of reduced ejection fraction, the treatment effect was consistent regardless of the presence or absence of diabetes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"Findings in patients with diabetes were similar to those in patients without diabetes."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"The effect of empagliflozin on the primary outcome was consistent in patients regardless of the presence or absence of diabetes."} --> <!-- evimed-statement:{"kind":"synthesized","confidence":"high"} -->

## Effect in heart failure with mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction

- In patients with class II-IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of more than 40%, empagliflozin 10 mg once daily reduced the composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure compared with placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"we randomly assigned 5988 patients with class II-IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of more than 40% to receive empagliflozin (10 mg once daily) or placebo, in addition to usual therapy. The primary outcome was a composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"a primary outcome event occurred in 415 of 2997 patients (13.8%) in the empagliflozin group and in 511 of 2991 patients (17.1%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.79; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.69 to 0.90; P\u003c0.001)"} -->
- In that trial the effect was mainly related to a lower risk of hospitalization for heart failure, and the total number of hospitalizations for heart failure was lower with empagliflozin. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"This effect was mainly related to a lower risk of hospitalization for heart failure in the empagliflozin group."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"The total number of hospitalizations for heart failure was lower in the empagliflozin group than in the placebo group (407 with empagliflozin and 541 with placebo; hazard ratio, 0.73; 95% CI, 0.61 to 0.88; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- In patients with heart failure and a left ventricular ejection fraction of more than 40%, dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily reduced the composite of worsening heart failure or cardiovascular death compared with placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"We randomly assigned 6263 patients with heart failure and a left ventricular ejection fraction of more than 40% to receive dapagliflozin (at a dose of 10 mg once daily) or matching placebo, in addition to usual therapy. The primary outcome was a composite of worsening heart failure (which was defined as either an unplanned hospitalization for heart failure or an urgent visit for heart failure) or cardiovascular death, as assessed in a time-to-event analysis."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"the primary outcome occurred in 512 of 3131 patients (16.4%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 610 of 3132 patients (19.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.82; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.73 to 0.92; P\u003c0.001)"} -->
- In that trial worsening heart failure occurred in 11.8% of the dapagliflozin group and 14.5% of the placebo group, while the hazard ratio for cardiovascular death was 0.88 with a confidence interval that included 1. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"Worsening heart failure occurred in 368 patients (11.8%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 455 patients (14.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.79; 95% CI, 0.69 to 0.91); cardiovascular death occurred in 231 patients (7.4%) and 261 patients (8.3%), respectively (hazard ratio, 0.88; 95% CI, 0.74 to 1.05)."} -->
- In the two trials of mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction, the effects appeared consistent in patients with or without diabetes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"The effects of empagliflozin appeared consistent in patients with or without diabetes."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"results were similar in prespecified subgroups, including patients with or without diabetes"} --> <!-- evimed-statement:{"kind":"synthesized","confidence":"high"} -->

## Combined evidence across the ejection fraction spectrum

- In a prespecified meta-analysis of DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved, SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the composite of cardiovascular death or first hospitalisation for heart failure among 12 251 participants, with consistent reductions in both components. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_4b1be72f0031135f81e0915e571e919b","quote":"Among 12 251 participants from DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved, SGLT2 inhibitors reduced composite cardiovascular death or first hospitalisation for heart failure (hazard ratio 0·80 [95% CI 0·73-0·87]) with consistent reductions in both components: cardiovascular death (0·88 [0·77-1·00]) and first hospitalisation for heart failure (0·74 [0·67-0·83])."} -->
- Across five trials of 21 947 participants, SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the risk of composite cardiovascular death or hospitalisation for heart failure, cardiovascular death, first hospitalisation for heart failure, and all-cause mortality. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_4b1be72f0031135f81e0915e571e919b","quote":"In the broader context of the five trials of 21 947 participants, SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the risk of composite cardiovascular death or hospitalisation for heart failure (0·77 [0·72-0·82]), cardiovascular death (0·87 [0·79-0·95]), first hospitalisation for heart failure (0·72 [0·67-0·78]), and all-cause mortality (0·92 [0·86-0·99])."} -->
- Treatment effects on the primary endpoint were generally consistent across the 14 subgroups examined, including ejection fraction. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_4b1be72f0031135f81e0915e571e919b","quote":"Treatment effects on the primary endpoint were generally consistent across the 14 subgroups examined, including ejection fraction."} -->

## Safety

- In DAPA-HF, the frequency of adverse events related to volume depletion, renal dysfunction, and hypoglycemia did not differ between the dapagliflozin and placebo groups. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"The frequency of adverse events related to volume depletion, renal dysfunction, and hypoglycemia did not differ between treatment groups."} -->
- In EMPEROR-Reduced, uncomplicated genital tract infection was reported more frequently with empagliflozin. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"Uncomplicated genital tract infection was reported more frequently with empagliflozin."} -->
- In EMPEROR-Preserved, uncomplicated genital and urinary tract infections and hypotension were reported more frequently with empagliflozin. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"Uncomplicated genital and urinary tract infections and hypotension were reported more frequently with empagliflozin."} -->
- In DELIVER, the incidence of adverse events was similar in the dapagliflozin and placebo groups. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"The incidence of adverse events was similar in the two groups."} -->
