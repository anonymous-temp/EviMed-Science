# Dapagliflozin

## Trials in heart failure

- In DAPA-HF, 4744 patients with New York Heart Association class II, III, or IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of 40% or less were randomly assigned to dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily or placebo in addition to recommended therapy. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"we randomly assigned 4744 patients with New York Heart Association class II, III, or IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of 40% or less to receive either dapagliflozin (at a dose of 10 mg once daily) or placebo, in addition to recommended therapy"} -->
- In DAPA-HF, the primary outcome was a composite of worsening heart failure (hospitalization or an urgent visit resulting in intravenous therapy for heart failure) or cardiovascular death. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"The primary outcome was a composite of worsening heart failure (hospitalization or an urgent visit resulting in intravenous therapy for heart failure) or cardiovascular death."} -->
- In DAPA-HF, findings in patients with diabetes were similar to those in patients without diabetes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"Findings in patients with diabetes were similar to those in patients without diabetes."} -->
- In DELIVER, 6263 patients with heart failure and a left ventricular ejection fraction of more than 40% were randomly assigned to dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily or matching placebo in addition to usual therapy. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"We randomly assigned 6263 patients with heart failure and a left ventricular ejection fraction of more than 40% to receive dapagliflozin (at a dose of 10 mg once daily) or matching placebo, in addition to usual therapy."} -->
- In DELIVER, results were similar among patients with a left ventricular ejection fraction of 60% or more and those with a left ventricular ejection fraction of less than 60%, and in prespecified subgroups including patients with or without diabetes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"Results were similar among patients with a left ventricular ejection fraction of 60% or more and those with a left ventricular ejection fraction of less than 60%, and results were similar in prespecified subgroups, including patients with or without diabetes."} -->

## Trials in chronic kidney disease

- In DAPA-CKD, 4304 participants with an estimated GFR of 25 to 75 ml per minute per 1.73 m2 and a urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio of 200 to 5000 were randomly assigned to dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily or placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"We randomly assigned 4304 participants with an estimated glomerular filtration rate (GFR) of 25 to 75 ml per minute per 1.73 m2 of body-surface area and a urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio (with albumin measured in milligrams and creatinine measured in grams) of 200 to 5000 to receive dapagliflozin (10 mg once daily) or placebo."} -->
- In DAPA-CKD, the independent data monitoring committee recommended stopping the trial because of efficacy. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"The independent data monitoring committee recommended stopping the trial because of efficacy."} -->
- In DAPA-CKD, death occurred in 101 participants (4.7%) in the dapagliflozin group and 146 participants (6.8%) in the placebo group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"Death occurred in 101 participants (4.7%) in the dapagliflozin group and 146 participants (6.8%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.69; 95% CI, 0.53 to 0.88; P = 0.004)."} -->
