# Edoxaban

## Efficacy and safety versus warfarin in atrial fibrillation (ENGAGE AF-TIMI 48)

- In the randomized, double-blind, double-dummy ENGAGE AF-TIMI 48 trial, two once-daily regimens of edoxaban were compared with warfarin in 21,105 patients with moderate-to-high-risk atrial fibrillation, with a median follow-up of 2.8 years. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_1bcbd58b429315ec591b7e167517461a","quote":"We conducted a randomized, double-blind, double-dummy trial comparing two once-daily regimens of edoxaban with warfarin in 21,105 patients with moderate-to-high-risk atrial fibrillation (median follow-up, 2.8 years)."} -->
- During treatment, the annualized rate of stroke or systemic embolism was 1.50% with warfarin (median time in the therapeutic range, 68.4%), 1.18% with high-dose edoxaban (hazard ratio 0.79; 97.5% CI, 0.63 to 0.99; P<0.001 for noninferiority) and 1.61% with low-dose edoxaban (hazard ratio 1.07; 97.5% CI, 0.87 to 1.31; P=0.005 for noninferiority). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_1bcbd58b429315ec591b7e167517461a","quote":"The annualized rate of the primary end point during treatment was 1.50% with warfarin (median time in the therapeutic range, 68.4%), as compared with 1.18% with high-dose edoxaban (hazard ratio, 0.79; 97.5% confidence interval [CI], 0.63 to 0.99; P\u003c0.001 for noninferiority) and 1.61% with low-dose edoxaban (hazard ratio, 1.07; 97.5% CI, 0.87 to 1.31; P=0.005 for noninferiority)."} -->
- The annualized rate of major bleeding was 3.43% with warfarin versus 2.75% with high-dose edoxaban (hazard ratio 0.80; 95% CI, 0.71 to 0.91; P<0.001) and 1.61% with low-dose edoxaban (hazard ratio 0.47; 95% CI, 0.41 to 0.55; P<0.001). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_1bcbd58b429315ec591b7e167517461a","quote":"The annualized rate of major bleeding was 3.43% with warfarin versus 2.75% with high-dose edoxaban (hazard ratio, 0.80; 95% CI, 0.71 to 0.91; P\u003c0.001) and 1.61% with low-dose edoxaban (hazard ratio, 0.47; 95% CI, 0.41 to 0.55; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- Annualized rates of death from cardiovascular causes were 3.17% with warfarin versus 2.74% with high-dose edoxaban (hazard ratio 0.86; 95% CI, 0.77 to 0.97; P=0.01) and 2.71% with low-dose edoxaban (hazard ratio 0.85; 95% CI, 0.76 to 0.96; P=0.008). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_1bcbd58b429315ec591b7e167517461a","quote":"The corresponding annualized rates of death from cardiovascular causes were 3.17% versus 2.74% (hazard ratio, 0.86; 95% CI, 0.77 to 0.97; P=0.01), and 2.71% (hazard ratio, 0.85; 95% CI, 0.76 to 0.96; P=0.008)"} -->
- Both once-daily regimens of edoxaban were noninferior to warfarin for prevention of stroke or systemic embolism and were associated with significantly lower rates of bleeding and death from cardiovascular causes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_1bcbd58b429315ec591b7e167517461a","quote":"Both once-daily regimens of edoxaban were noninferior to warfarin with respect to the prevention of stroke or systemic embolism and were associated with significantly lower rates of bleeding and death from cardiovascular causes."} -->

## Low-dose edoxaban in very elderly patients (ELDERCARE-AF)

- ELDERCARE-AF was a phase 3, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, event-driven trial of once-daily edoxaban 15 mg versus placebo in elderly Japanese patients (≥80 years of age) with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation who were not considered appropriate candidates for oral anticoagulant therapy at approved stroke-prevention doses. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_0e9d75e95b4aa172897de8e3f4333228","quote":"We conducted a phase 3, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, event-driven trial to compare a once-daily 15-mg dose of edoxaban with placebo in elderly Japanese patients (≥80 years of age) with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation who were not considered to be appropriate candidates for oral anticoagulant therapy at doses approved for stroke prevention."} -->
- A total of 984 patients were randomly assigned 1:1 to edoxaban 15 mg daily (492 patients) or placebo (492 patients); 681 completed the trial and 303 discontinued. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_0e9d75e95b4aa172897de8e3f4333228","quote":"A total of 984 patients were randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio to receive a daily dose of 15 mg of edoxaban (492 patients) or placebo (492 patients). A total of 681 patients completed the trial, and 303 discontinued (158 withdrew, 135 died, and 10 had other reasons); the numbers of patients who discontinued the trial were similar in the two groups."} -->
- The annualized rate of stroke or systemic embolism was 2.3% with edoxaban and 6.7% with placebo (hazard ratio 0.34; 95% CI, 0.19 to 0.61; P<0.001), and the annualized rate of major bleeding was 3.3% with edoxaban and 1.8% with placebo (hazard ratio 1.87; 95% CI, 0.90 to 3.89; P=0.09). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_0e9d75e95b4aa172897de8e3f4333228","quote":"The annualized rate of stroke or systemic embolism was 2.3% in the edoxaban group and 6.7% in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.34; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.19 to 0.61; P\u003c0.001), and the annualized rate of major bleeding was 3.3% in the edoxaban group and 1.8% in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 1.87; 95% CI, 0.90 to 3.89; P = 0.09)."} -->
- There were substantially more events of gastrointestinal bleeding in the edoxaban group than in the placebo group, and no substantial between-group difference in death from any cause (9.9% with edoxaban and 10.2% with placebo; hazard ratio 0.97; 95% CI, 0.69 to 1.36). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_0e9d75e95b4aa172897de8e3f4333228","quote":"There were substantially more events of gastrointestinal bleeding in the edoxaban group than in the placebo group. There was no substantial between-group difference in death from any cause (9.9% in the edoxaban group and 10.2% in the placebo group; hazard ratio, 0.97; 95% CI, 0.69 to 1.36)."} -->
- In very elderly Japanese patients with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation who were not appropriate candidates for standard doses of oral anticoagulants, once-daily edoxaban 15 mg was superior to placebo in preventing stroke or systemic embolism and did not result in a significantly higher incidence of major bleeding than placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_0e9d75e95b4aa172897de8e3f4333228","quote":"In very elderly Japanese patients with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation who were not appropriate candidates for standard doses of oral anticoagulants, a once-daily 15-mg dose of edoxaban was superior to placebo in preventing stroke or systemic embolism and did not result in a significantly higher incidence of major bleeding than placebo."} -->
