# Dabigatran

## Efficacy and safety versus warfarin in atrial fibrillation

- In the RE-LY noninferiority trial, 18,113 patients with atrial fibrillation and a risk of stroke were randomly assigned to fixed doses of dabigatran 110 mg or 150 mg twice daily or to adjusted-dose warfarin, with a median follow-up of 2.0 years. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8a669b066c4cb002e5224ef48c9a8550","quote":"In this noninferiority trial, we randomly assigned 18,113 patients who had atrial fibrillation and a risk of stroke to receive, in a blinded fashion, fixed doses of dabigatran--110 mg or 150 mg twice daily--or, in an unblinded fashion, adjusted-dose warfarin. The median duration of the follow-up period was 2.0 years."} -->
- The rate of stroke or systemic embolism was 1.69% per year with warfarin, 1.53% per year with dabigatran 110 mg (relative risk 0.91; 95% CI, 0.74 to 1.11; P<0.001 for noninferiority) and 1.11% per year with dabigatran 150 mg (relative risk 0.66; 95% CI, 0.53 to 0.82; P<0.001 for superiority). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8a669b066c4cb002e5224ef48c9a8550","quote":"Rates of the primary outcome were 1.69% per year in the warfarin group, as compared with 1.53% per year in the group that received 110 mg of dabigatran (relative risk with dabigatran, 0.91; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.74 to 1.11; P\u003c0.001 for noninferiority) and 1.11% per year in the group that received 150 mg of dabigatran (relative risk, 0.66; 95% CI, 0.53 to 0.82; P\u003c0.001 for superiority)."} -->
- Major bleeding occurred at 3.36% per year with warfarin, 2.71% per year with dabigatran 110 mg (P=0.003) and 3.11% per year with dabigatran 150 mg (P=0.31). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8a669b066c4cb002e5224ef48c9a8550","quote":"The rate of major bleeding was 3.36% per year in the warfarin group, as compared with 2.71% per year in the group receiving 110 mg of dabigatran (P=0.003) and 3.11% per year in the group receiving 150 mg of dabigatran (P=0.31)."} -->
- Hemorrhagic stroke occurred at 0.38% per year with warfarin versus 0.12% per year with dabigatran 110 mg (P<0.001) and 0.10% per year with dabigatran 150 mg (P<0.001). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8a669b066c4cb002e5224ef48c9a8550","quote":"The rate of hemorrhagic stroke was 0.38% per year in the warfarin group, as compared with 0.12% per year with 110 mg of dabigatran (P\u003c0.001) and 0.10% per year with 150 mg of dabigatran (P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- Mortality was 4.13% per year with warfarin, 3.75% per year with dabigatran 110 mg (P=0.13) and 3.64% per year with dabigatran 150 mg (P=0.051). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8a669b066c4cb002e5224ef48c9a8550","quote":"The mortality rate was 4.13% per year in the warfarin group, as compared with 3.75% per year with 110 mg of dabigatran (P=0.13) and 3.64% per year with 150 mg of dabigatran (P=0.051)."} -->
- In patients with atrial fibrillation, dabigatran 110 mg was associated with rates of stroke and systemic embolism similar to warfarin and lower rates of major hemorrhage, whereas dabigatran 150 mg was associated with lower rates of stroke and systemic embolism but similar rates of major hemorrhage. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8a669b066c4cb002e5224ef48c9a8550","quote":"In patients with atrial fibrillation, dabigatran given at a dose of 110 mg was associated with rates of stroke and systemic embolism that were similar to those associated with warfarin, as well as lower rates of major hemorrhage. Dabigatran administered at a dose of 150 mg, as compared with warfarin, was associated with lower rates of stroke and systemic embolism but similar rates of major hemorrhage."} -->
