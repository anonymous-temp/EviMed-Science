# Oral anticoagulation in elderly and Japanese patients with atrial fibrillation

## Very elderly patients unsuitable for standard doses

- Implementation of appropriate oral anticoagulant treatment for stroke prevention in very elderly patients with atrial fibrillation is challenging because of concerns regarding bleeding. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_0e9d75e95b4aa172897de8e3f4333228","quote":"Implementation of appropriate oral anticoagulant treatment for the prevention of stroke in very elderly patients with atrial fibrillation is challenging because of concerns regarding bleeding."} -->
- In very elderly Japanese patients with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation who were not appropriate candidates for standard doses of oral anticoagulants, once-daily edoxaban 15 mg was superior to placebo in preventing stroke or systemic embolism and did not result in a significantly higher incidence of major bleeding than placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_0e9d75e95b4aa172897de8e3f4333228","quote":"In very elderly Japanese patients with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation who were not appropriate candidates for standard doses of oral anticoagulants, a once-daily 15-mg dose of edoxaban was superior to placebo in preventing stroke or systemic embolism and did not result in a significantly higher incidence of major bleeding than placebo."} -->

## Japan-specific dosing

- J-ROCKET AF compared a Japan-specific rivaroxaban dose of 15 mg once daily with warfarin dose-adjusted according to Japanese guidelines in Japanese patients with atrial fibrillation, and demonstrated the safety of that Japan-specific dose. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81808a7c4c3bf0e34bac1c720768f46d","quote":"A separate trial, J-ROCKET AF, compared the safety of a Japan-specific rivaroxaban dose with warfarin administered according to Japanese guidelines in Japanese patients with AF."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81808a7c4c3bf0e34bac1c720768f46d","quote":"J-ROCKET AF demonstrated the safety of a Japan-specific rivaroxaban dose and supports bridging the global ROCKET AF results into Japanese clinical practice."} -->
