# DAPA-CKD trial

## Design

- DAPA-CKD randomly assigned 4304 participants with an estimated GFR of 25 to 75 ml per minute per 1.73 m2 and a urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio of 200 to 5000 to dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily or placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"We randomly assigned 4304 participants with an estimated glomerular filtration rate (GFR) of 25 to 75 ml per minute per 1.73 m2 of body-surface area and a urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio (with albumin measured in milligrams and creatinine measured in grams) of 200 to 5000 to receive dapagliflozin (10 mg once daily) or placebo."} -->
- The primary outcome was a composite of a sustained decline in the estimated GFR of at least 50%, end-stage kidney disease, or death from renal or cardiovascular causes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"The primary outcome was a composite of a sustained decline in the estimated GFR of at least 50%, end-stage kidney disease, or death from renal or cardiovascular causes."} -->

## Conduct and result

- The independent data monitoring committee recommended stopping the trial because of efficacy. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"The independent data monitoring committee recommended stopping the trial because of efficacy."} -->
- Over a median of 2.4 years, a primary outcome event occurred in 197 of 2152 participants (9.2%) in the dapagliflozin group and 312 of 2152 participants (14.5%) in the placebo group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"Over a median of 2.4 years, a primary outcome event occurred in 197 of 2152 participants (9.2%) in the dapagliflozin group and 312 of 2152 participants (14.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.61; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.51 to 0.72; P\u003c0.001; number needed to treat to prevent one primary outcome event, 19 [95% CI, 15 to 27])."} -->
- The known safety profile of dapagliflozin was confirmed. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_01c40038ab279aadbaf12401aadbad29","quote":"The known safety profile of dapagliflozin was confirmed."} -->
