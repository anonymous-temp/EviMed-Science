# New oral anticoagulants versus warfarin in atrial fibrillation

## Pooled efficacy and safety (meta-analysis of RE-LY, ROCKET AF, ARISTOTLE and ENGAGE AF-TIMI 48)

- A prespecified meta-analysis pooled all 71,683 participants of the phase 3 randomised trials RE-LY, ROCKET AF, ARISTOTLE and ENGAGE AF-TIMI 48, of whom 42,411 received a new oral anticoagulant and 29,272 received warfarin. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_68f0cec57cbc9a18bfbd4cfce07d3b63","quote":"We did a prespecified meta-analysis of all 71,683 participants included in the RE-LY, ROCKET AF, ARISTOTLE, and ENGAGE AF-TIMI 48 trials."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_68f0cec57cbc9a18bfbd4cfce07d3b63","quote":"42,411 participants received a new oral anticoagulant and 29,272 participants received warfarin."} -->
- New oral anticoagulants significantly reduced stroke or systemic embolic events by 19% compared with warfarin (RR 0.81, 95% CI 0.73-0.91; p<0.0001), mainly driven by a reduction in haemorrhagic stroke (0.49, 0.38-0.64; p<0.0001). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_68f0cec57cbc9a18bfbd4cfce07d3b63","quote":"New oral anticoagulants significantly reduced stroke or systemic embolic events by 19% compared with warfarin (RR 0·81, 95% CI 0·73-0·91; p\u003c0·0001), mainly driven by a reduction in haemorrhagic stroke (0·49, 0·38-0·64; p\u003c0·0001)."} -->
- New oral anticoagulants also significantly reduced all-cause mortality (0.90, 0.85-0.95; p=0.0003) and intracranial haemorrhage (0.48, 0.39-0.59; p<0.0001), but increased gastrointestinal bleeding (1.25, 1.01-1.55; p=0.04). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_68f0cec57cbc9a18bfbd4cfce07d3b63","quote":"New oral anticoagulants also significantly reduced all-cause mortality (0·90, 0·85-0·95; p=0·0003) and intracranial haemorrhage (0·48, 0·39-0·59; p\u003c0·0001), but increased gastrointestinal bleeding (1·25, 1·01-1·55; p=0·04)."} -->
- There was no heterogeneity for stroke or systemic embolic events in important subgroups, but the relative reduction in major bleeding with new oral anticoagulants was greater when the centre-based time in therapeutic range was less than 66% than when it was 66% or more (0.69, 0.59-0.81 vs 0.93, 0.76-1.13; p for interaction 0.022). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_68f0cec57cbc9a18bfbd4cfce07d3b63","quote":"We noted no heterogeneity for stroke or systemic embolic events in important subgroups, but there was a greater relative reduction in major bleeding with new oral anticoagulants when the centre-based time in therapeutic range was less than 66% than when it was 66% or more (0·69, 0·59-0·81 vs 0·93, 0·76-1·13; p for interaction 0·022)."} -->
- Low-dose new oral anticoagulant regimens showed similar overall reductions in stroke or systemic embolic events to warfarin (1.03, 0.84-1.27; p=0.74) and a more favourable bleeding profile (0.65, 0.43-1.00; p=0.05), but significantly more ischaemic strokes (1.28, 1.02-1.60; p=0.045). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_68f0cec57cbc9a18bfbd4cfce07d3b63","quote":"Low-dose new oral anticoagulant regimens showed similar overall reductions in stroke or systemic embolic events to warfarin (1·03, 0·84-1·27; p=0·74), and a more favourable bleeding profile (0·65, 0·43-1·00; p=0·05), but significantly more ischaemic strokes (1·28, 1·02-1·60; p=0·045)."} -->

## Individual trial results against warfarin

- Across the individual trials, dabigatran 110 mg and 150 mg, rivaroxaban 20 mg, apixaban 5 mg and both edoxaban regimens were each noninferior to warfarin for stroke or systemic embolism in patients with atrial fibrillation. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8a669b066c4cb002e5224ef48c9a8550","quote":"In patients with atrial fibrillation, dabigatran given at a dose of 110 mg was associated with rates of stroke and systemic embolism that were similar to those associated with warfarin"} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8ff4bad2e9d926335b4de77ad1e41630","quote":"In patients with atrial fibrillation, rivaroxaban was noninferior to warfarin for the prevention of stroke or systemic embolism."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81e343c146c92d15d7c2f15b65748ce1","quote":"In patients with atrial fibrillation, apixaban was superior to warfarin in preventing stroke or systemic embolism"} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_1bcbd58b429315ec591b7e167517461a","quote":"Both once-daily regimens of edoxaban were noninferior to warfarin with respect to the prevention of stroke or systemic embolism"} --> <!-- evimed-statement:{"kind":"synthesized","confidence":"high"} -->
- Bleeding outcomes differed by agent and dose: dabigatran 110 mg and both edoxaban regimens had lower major bleeding rates than warfarin, apixaban caused less bleeding, rivaroxaban showed no significant difference in major bleeding but less intracranial and fatal bleeding, and dabigatran 150 mg had a similar rate of major hemorrhage to warfarin. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8a669b066c4cb002e5224ef48c9a8550","quote":"Dabigatran given at a dose of 110 mg was associated with rates of stroke and systemic embolism that were similar to those associated with warfarin, as well as lower rates of major hemorrhage. Dabigatran administered at a dose of 150 mg, as compared with warfarin, was associated with lower rates of stroke and systemic embolism but similar rates of major hemorrhage."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8ff4bad2e9d926335b4de77ad1e41630","quote":"There was no significant between-group difference in the risk of major bleeding, although intracranial and fatal bleeding occurred less frequently in the rivaroxaban group."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81e343c146c92d15d7c2f15b65748ce1","quote":"apixaban was superior to warfarin in preventing stroke or systemic embolism, caused less bleeding, and resulted in lower mortality"} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_1bcbd58b429315ec591b7e167517461a","quote":"Both once-daily regimens of edoxaban were noninferior to warfarin with respect to the prevention of stroke or systemic embolism and were associated with significantly lower rates of bleeding and death from cardiovascular causes."} --> <!-- evimed-statement:{"kind":"synthesized","confidence":"moderate"} -->
