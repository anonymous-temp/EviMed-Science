# Canagliflozin

## Identity and use

- Canagliflozin is an oral SGLT2 inhibitor given at a dose of 100 mg daily in a trial of patients with type 2 diabetes and albuminuric chronic kidney disease. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_57ce472296b677f0d08707b04c50a8f0","quote":"to receive canagliflozin, an oral SGLT2 inhibitor, at a dose of 100 mg daily or placebo"} -->

## Renal and cardiovascular outcomes

- In patients with type 2 diabetes and kidney disease, the risk of kidney failure and cardiovascular events was lower in the canagliflozin group than in the placebo group at a median follow-up of 2.62 years. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_57ce472296b677f0d08707b04c50a8f0","quote":"In patients with type 2 diabetes and kidney disease, the risk of kidney failure and cardiovascular events was lower in the canagliflozin group than in the placebo group at a median follow-up of 2.62 years."} -->
- In that trial there were no significant differences in rates of amputation or fracture. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_57ce472296b677f0d08707b04c50a8f0","quote":"There were no significant differences in rates of amputation or fracture."} -->
