# SGLT2 inhibitors in heart failure

## Effect in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction

- In patients with heart failure and an ejection fraction of 40% or less, dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily reduced the composite of worsening heart failure or cardiovascular death compared with placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"the primary outcome occurred in 386 of 2373 patients (16.3%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 502 of 2371 patients (21.2%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.74; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.65 to 0.85; P\u003c0.001)"} -->
- In the same trial, a first worsening heart failure event occurred less often with dapagliflozin than with placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"A first worsening heart failure event occurred in 237 patients (10.0%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 326 patients (13.7%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.70; 95% CI, 0.59 to 0.83)."} -->
- Death from cardiovascular causes occurred in 227 patients (9.6%) with dapagliflozin and 273 patients (11.5%) with placebo, and death from any cause in 276 patients (11.6%) and 329 patients (13.9%), respectively. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"Death from cardiovascular causes occurred in 227 patients (9.6%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 273 patients (11.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.82; 95% CI, 0.69 to 0.98); 276 patients (11.6%) and 329 patients (13.9%), respectively, died from any cause (hazard ratio, 0.83; 95% CI, 0.71 to 0.97)."} -->
- In patients with class II, III, or IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of 40% or less, empagliflozin 10 mg once daily reduced the composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for worsening heart failure compared with placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"a primary outcome event occurred in 361 of 1863 patients (19.4%) in the empagliflozin group and in 462 of 1867 patients (24.7%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio for cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure, 0.75; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.65 to 0.86; P\u003c0.001)"} -->
- In that trial the total number of hospitalizations for heart failure was lower with empagliflozin than with placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"The total number of hospitalizations for heart failure was lower in the empagliflozin group than in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.70; 95% CI, 0.58 to 0.85; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- In patients with heart failure and a reduced ejection fraction, the benefit of dapagliflozin on worsening heart failure or cardiovascular death was observed regardless of the presence or absence of diabetes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"the risk of worsening heart failure or death from cardiovascular causes was lower among those who received dapagliflozin than among those who received placebo, regardless of the presence or absence of diabetes"} -->
- In patients with heart failure and a reduced ejection fraction, the effect of empagliflozin on the primary outcome was consistent regardless of the presence or absence of diabetes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"The effect of empagliflozin on the primary outcome was consistent in patients regardless of the presence or absence of diabetes."} -->

## Effect in heart failure with mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction

- In patients with class II-IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of more than 40%, empagliflozin 10 mg once daily reduced the composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure compared with placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"a primary outcome event occurred in 415 of 2997 patients (13.8%) in the empagliflozin group and in 511 of 2991 patients (17.1%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.79; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.69 to 0.90; P\u003c0.001)"} -->
- In that trial the effect was mainly related to a lower risk of hospitalization for heart failure with empagliflozin. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"This effect was mainly related to a lower risk of hospitalization for heart failure in the empagliflozin group."} -->
- In patients with heart failure and a left ventricular ejection fraction of more than 40%, dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily reduced the composite of worsening heart failure or cardiovascular death compared with placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"the primary outcome occurred in 512 of 3131 patients (16.4%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 610 of 3132 patients (19.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.82; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.73 to 0.92; P\u003c0.001)"} -->
- In that trial worsening heart failure occurred in 368 patients (11.8%) with dapagliflozin and 455 patients (14.5%) with placebo, while cardiovascular death occurred in 231 patients (7.4%) and 261 patients (8.3%), respectively. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"Worsening heart failure occurred in 368 patients (11.8%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 455 patients (14.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.79; 95% CI, 0.69 to 0.91); cardiovascular death occurred in 231 patients (7.4%) and 261 patients (8.3%), respectively (hazard ratio, 0.88; 95% CI, 0.74 to 1.05)."} -->
- In patients with heart failure and a preserved ejection fraction, the effect of empagliflozin appeared consistent in those with or without diabetes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"The effects of empagliflozin appeared consistent in patients with or without diabetes."} -->
- In patients with heart failure and a mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction, results with dapagliflozin were similar in prespecified subgroups, including patients with or without diabetes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"results were similar in prespecified subgroups, including patients with or without diabetes"} -->

## Pooled effects across the trial programme

- Among 12 251 participants from DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved, SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the composite of cardiovascular death or first hospitalisation for heart failure, with consistent reductions in both components. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_4b1be72f0031135f81e0915e571e919b","quote":"Among 12 251 participants from DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved, SGLT2 inhibitors reduced composite cardiovascular death or first hospitalisation for heart failure (hazard ratio 0·80 [95% CI 0·73-0·87]) with consistent reductions in both components: cardiovascular death (0·88 [0·77-1·00]) and first hospitalisation for heart failure (0·74 [0·67-0·83])."} -->
- Across the five trials of 21 947 participants, SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the risk of composite cardiovascular death or hospitalisation for heart failure, cardiovascular death, first hospitalisation for heart failure, and all-cause mortality. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_4b1be72f0031135f81e0915e571e919b","quote":"In the broader context of the five trials of 21 947 participants, SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the risk of composite cardiovascular death or hospitalisation for heart failure (0·77 [0·72-0·82]), cardiovascular death (0·87 [0·79-0·95]), first hospitalisation for heart failure (0·72 [0·67-0·78]), and all-cause mortality (0·92 [0·86-0·99])."} -->
- In the meta-analysis, treatment effects on the primary endpoint were generally consistent across the 14 subgroups examined, including ejection fraction. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_4b1be72f0031135f81e0915e571e919b","quote":"Treatment effects on the primary endpoint were generally consistent across the 14 subgroups examined, including ejection fraction."} -->

## Safety

- In patients with heart failure and a reduced ejection fraction, the frequency of adverse events related to volume depletion, renal dysfunction, and hypoglycemia did not differ between dapagliflozin and placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"The frequency of adverse events related to volume depletion, renal dysfunction, and hypoglycemia did not differ between treatment groups."} -->
- Uncomplicated genital tract infection was reported more frequently with empagliflozin in patients with heart failure and a reduced ejection fraction. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"Uncomplicated genital tract infection was reported more frequently with empagliflozin."} -->
- In patients with heart failure and a preserved ejection fraction, uncomplicated genital and urinary tract infections and hypotension were reported more frequently with empagliflozin. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"Uncomplicated genital and urinary tract infections and hypotension were reported more frequently with empagliflozin."} -->
- In patients with heart failure and a mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction, the incidence of adverse events was similar in the dapagliflozin and placebo groups. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"The incidence of adverse events was similar in the two groups."} -->
