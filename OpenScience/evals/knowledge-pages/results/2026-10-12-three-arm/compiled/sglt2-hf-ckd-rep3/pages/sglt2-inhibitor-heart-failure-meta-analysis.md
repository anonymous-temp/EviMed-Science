# Meta-analysis of five randomised controlled trials of SGLT-2 inhibitors in heart failure

## Scope and method

- The meta-analysis was prespecified for DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved and subsequently included trials enrolling patients with reduced ejection fraction (DAPA-HF and EMPEROR-Reduced) and those admitted to hospital with worsening heart failure irrespective of ejection fraction (SOLOIST-WHF). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_4b1be72f0031135f81e0915e571e919b","quote":"We did a prespecified meta-analysis of DELIVER and EMPEROR-Preserved, and subsequently included trials that enrolled patients with reduced ejection fraction (DAPA-HF and EMPEROR-Reduced) and those admitted to hospital with worsening heart failure, irrespective of ejection fraction (SOLOIST-WHF)."} -->
- Using trial-level data with harmonised endpoint definitions, a fixed-effects meta-analysis estimated the effect of SGLT2 inhibitors on clinical endpoints in heart failure, with the composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalisation for heart failure as the primary endpoint. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_4b1be72f0031135f81e0915e571e919b","quote":"Using trial-level data with harmonised endpoint definitions, we did a fixed-effects meta-analysis to estimate the effect of SGLT2 inhibitors on various clinical endpoints in heart failure The primary endpoint for this meta-analysis was time from randomisation to the occurrence of the composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalisation for heart failure."} -->

## Findings

- These treatment effects for each of the studied endpoints were consistently observed in both the trials of heart failure with mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction and across all five trials. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_4b1be72f0031135f81e0915e571e919b","quote":"These treatment effects for each of the studied endpoints were consistently observed in both the trials of heart failure with mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction and across all five trials."} -->
