# DELIVER trial

## Design

- DELIVER randomly assigned 6263 patients with heart failure and a left ventricular ejection fraction of more than 40% to dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily or matching placebo, in addition to usual therapy. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"We randomly assigned 6263 patients with heart failure and a left ventricular ejection fraction of more than 40% to receive dapagliflozin (at a dose of 10 mg once daily) or matching placebo, in addition to usual therapy."} -->
- The primary outcome was a composite of worsening heart failure (an unplanned hospitalization for heart failure or an urgent visit for heart failure) or cardiovascular death, assessed in a time-to-event analysis. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"The primary outcome was a composite of worsening heart failure (which was defined as either an unplanned hospitalization for heart failure or an urgent visit for heart failure) or cardiovascular death, as assessed in a time-to-event analysis."} -->

## Result

- Over a median of 2.3 years, the primary outcome occurred in 512 of 3131 patients (16.4%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 610 of 3132 patients (19.5%) in the placebo group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"Over a median of 2.3 years, the primary outcome occurred in 512 of 3131 patients (16.4%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 610 of 3132 patients (19.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.82; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.73 to 0.92; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- Total events and symptom burden were lower in the dapagliflozin group than in the placebo group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"Total events and symptom burden were lower in the dapagliflozin group than in the placebo group."} -->
- Results were similar among patients with a left ventricular ejection fraction of 60% or more and those with a left ventricular ejection fraction of less than 60%. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"Results were similar among patients with a left ventricular ejection fraction of 60% or more and those with a left ventricular ejection fraction of less than 60%"} -->
