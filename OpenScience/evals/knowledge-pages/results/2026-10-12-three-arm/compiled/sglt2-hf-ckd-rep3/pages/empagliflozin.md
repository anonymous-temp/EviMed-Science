# Empagliflozin

## Identity and use

- Empagliflozin is a sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 inhibitor given at a dose of 10 mg once daily in the heart failure and chronic kidney disease trials. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"to receive empagliflozin (10 mg once daily) or placebo, in addition to recommended therapy"} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_9babb063478831150659a9e2e6b0e443","quote":"Patients were randomly assigned to receive empagliflozin (10 mg once daily) or matching placebo."} --> <!-- evimed-statement:{"kind":"synthesized","confidence":"high"} -->

## Heart failure with reduced ejection fraction

- Among patients receiving recommended therapy for heart failure, those in the empagliflozin group had a lower risk of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure than those in the placebo group, regardless of the presence or absence of diabetes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"Among patients receiving recommended therapy for heart failure, those in the empagliflozin group had a lower risk of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure than those in the placebo group, regardless of the presence or absence of diabetes."} -->

## Heart failure with preserved ejection fraction

- Empagliflozin reduced the combined risk of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure in patients with heart failure and a preserved ejection fraction, regardless of the presence or absence of diabetes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"Empagliflozin reduced the combined risk of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure in patients with heart failure and a preserved ejection fraction, regardless of the presence or absence of diabetes."} -->

## Chronic kidney disease

- Among a wide range of patients with chronic kidney disease who were at risk for disease progression, empagliflozin therapy led to a lower risk of progression of kidney disease or death from cardiovascular causes than placebo. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_9babb063478831150659a9e2e6b0e443","quote":"Among a wide range of patients with chronic kidney disease who were at risk for disease progression, empagliflozin therapy led to a lower risk of progression of kidney disease or death from cardiovascular causes than placebo."} -->
