# EMPEROR-Preserved

## Design

- EMPEROR-Preserved was a double-blind trial that randomly assigned 5988 patients with class II-IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of more than 40% to empagliflozin 10 mg once daily or placebo, in addition to usual therapy. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"In this double-blind trial, we randomly assigned 5988 patients with class II-IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of more than 40% to receive empagliflozin (10 mg once daily) or placebo, in addition to usual therapy."} -->
- The primary outcome was a composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"The primary outcome was a composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure."} -->

## Results

- Over a median of 26.2 months, a primary outcome event occurred in 13.8% of the empagliflozin group and 17.1% of the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.79; 95% CI, 0.69 to 0.90; P<0.001). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"Over a median of 26.2 months, a primary outcome event occurred in 415 of 2997 patients (13.8%) in the empagliflozin group and in 511 of 2991 patients (17.1%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.79; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.69 to 0.90; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- The total number of hospitalizations for heart failure was lower with empagliflozin than with placebo (407 vs. 541; hazard ratio, 0.73; 95% CI, 0.61 to 0.88; P<0.001). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"The total number of hospitalizations for heart failure was lower in the empagliflozin group than in the placebo group (407 with empagliflozin and 541 with placebo; hazard ratio, 0.73; 95% CI, 0.61 to 0.88; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
