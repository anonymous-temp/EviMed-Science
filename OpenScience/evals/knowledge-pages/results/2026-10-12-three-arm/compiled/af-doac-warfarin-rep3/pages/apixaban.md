# Apixaban

## ARISTOTLE: apixaban 5 mg twice daily versus warfarin

- In the randomized, double-blind ARISTOTLE trial, apixaban 5 mg twice daily was compared with warfarin (target international normalized ratio 2.0 to 3.0) in 18,201 patients with atrial fibrillation and at least one additional risk factor for stroke, with a median follow-up of 1.8 years. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81e343c146c92d15d7c2f15b65748ce1","quote":"In this randomized, double-blind trial, we compared apixaban (at a dose of 5 mg twice daily) with warfarin (target international normalized ratio, 2.0 to 3.0) in 18,201 patients with atrial fibrillation and at least one additional risk factor for stroke. The primary outcome was ischemic or hemorrhagic stroke or systemic embolism."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81e343c146c92d15d7c2f15b65748ce1","quote":"The median duration of follow-up was 1.8 years."} -->
- The rate of the primary outcome was 1.27% per year with apixaban versus 1.60% per year with warfarin (hazard ratio 0.79; 95% CI 0.66 to 0.95; P<0.001 for noninferiority; P=0.01 for superiority). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81e343c146c92d15d7c2f15b65748ce1","quote":"The rate of the primary outcome was 1.27% per year in the apixaban group, as compared with 1.60% per year in the warfarin group (hazard ratio with apixaban, 0.79; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.66 to 0.95; P\u003c0.001 for noninferiority; P=0.01 for superiority)."} -->
- Major bleeding occurred at 2.13% per year with apixaban versus 3.09% per year with warfarin (hazard ratio 0.69; 95% CI 0.60 to 0.80; P<0.001), and death from any cause at 3.52% and 3.94%, respectively (hazard ratio 0.89; 95% CI 0.80 to 0.99; P=0.047). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81e343c146c92d15d7c2f15b65748ce1","quote":"The rate of major bleeding was 2.13% per year in the apixaban group, as compared with 3.09% per year in the warfarin group (hazard ratio, 0.69; 95% CI, 0.60 to 0.80; P\u003c0.001), and the rates of death from any cause were 3.52% and 3.94%, respectively (hazard ratio, 0.89; 95% CI, 0.80 to 0.99; P=0.047)."} -->
- Hemorrhagic stroke occurred at 0.24% per year with apixaban versus 0.47% per year with warfarin (hazard ratio 0.51; 95% CI 0.35 to 0.75; P<0.001), while ischemic or uncertain type of stroke occurred at 0.97% per year with apixaban and 1.05% per year with warfarin (hazard ratio 0.92; 95% CI 0.74 to 1.13; P=0.42). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81e343c146c92d15d7c2f15b65748ce1","quote":"The rate of hemorrhagic stroke was 0.24% per year in the apixaban group, as compared with 0.47% per year in the warfarin group (hazard ratio, 0.51; 95% CI, 0.35 to 0.75; P\u003c0.001), and the rate of ischemic or uncertain type of stroke was 0.97% per year in the apixaban group and 1.05% per year in the warfarin group (hazard ratio, 0.92; 95% CI, 0.74 to 1.13; P=0.42)."} -->
- In patients with atrial fibrillation, apixaban was superior to warfarin in preventing stroke or systemic embolism, caused less bleeding, and resulted in lower mortality. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81e343c146c92d15d7c2f15b65748ce1","quote":"In patients with atrial fibrillation, apixaban was superior to warfarin in preventing stroke or systemic embolism, caused less bleeding, and resulted in lower mortality."} -->

## AVERROES: apixaban versus aspirin when vitamin K antagonist therapy is unsuitable

- In a double-blind study, 5,599 patients with atrial fibrillation at increased risk for stroke for whom vitamin K antagonist therapy was unsuitable were randomly assigned to apixaban 5 mg twice daily or aspirin 81 to 324 mg per day, with a mean follow-up of 1.1 years. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_ee77d1914d18a5ece9857b4e40a81405","quote":"In a double-blind study, we randomly assigned 5599 patients with atrial fibrillation who were at increased risk for stroke and for whom vitamin K antagonist therapy was unsuitable to receive apixaban (at a dose of 5 mg twice daily) or aspirin (81 to 324 mg per day), to determine whether apixaban was superior. The mean follow up period was 1.1 years."} -->
- The study was terminated early on the recommendation of the data and safety monitoring board because of a clear benefit in favor of apixaban. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_ee77d1914d18a5ece9857b4e40a81405","quote":"The data and safety monitoring board recommended early termination of the study because of a clear benefit in favor of apixaban."} -->
- There were 51 primary outcome events (1.6% per year) among patients assigned to apixaban and 113 (3.7% per year) among those assigned to aspirin (hazard ratio 0.45; 95% CI 0.32 to 0.62; P<0.001). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_ee77d1914d18a5ece9857b4e40a81405","quote":"There were 51 primary outcome events (1.6% per year) among patients assigned to apixaban and 113 (3.7% per year) among those assigned to aspirin (hazard ratio with apixaban, 0.45; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.32 to 0.62; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- Major bleeding occurred in 44 cases (1.4% per year) with apixaban and 39 (1.2% per year) with aspirin (hazard ratio 1.13; 95% CI 0.74 to 1.75; P=0.57), and there were 11 cases of intracranial bleeding with apixaban and 13 with aspirin. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_ee77d1914d18a5ece9857b4e40a81405","quote":"There were 44 cases of major bleeding (1.4% per year) in the apixaban group and 39 (1.2% per year) in the aspirin group (hazard ratio with apixaban, 1.13; 95% CI, 0.74 to 1.75; P=0.57); there were 11 cases of intracranial bleeding with apixaban and 13 with aspirin."} -->
- In patients with atrial fibrillation for whom vitamin K antagonist therapy was unsuitable, apixaban reduced the risk of stroke or systemic embolism without significantly increasing the risk of major bleeding or intracranial hemorrhage. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_ee77d1914d18a5ece9857b4e40a81405","quote":"In patients with atrial fibrillation for whom vitamin K antagonist therapy was unsuitable, apixaban reduced the risk of stroke or systemic embolism without significantly increasing the risk of major bleeding or intracranial hemorrhage."} -->
