# ARRIVE trial

## Design and results

- ARRIVE was a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicentre study in seven countries that enrolled patients aged 55 years (men) or 60 years (women) and older with moderate estimated cardiovascular risk, excluding those at high bleeding risk or with diabetes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_a80cd40ea6db9ff8486b4ad6e10638d2","quote":"ARRIVE is a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicentre study done in seven countries. Eligible patients were aged 55 years (men) or 60 years (women) and older and had an average cardiovascular risk, deemed to be moderate on the basis of the number of specific risk factors. We excluded patients at high risk of gastrointestinal bleeding or other bleeding, or diabetes."} -->
- Between July 5, 2007, and Nov 15, 2016, 12,546 patients were enrolled at 501 study sites and randomly assigned to aspirin (n=6270) or placebo (n=6276), with a median follow-up of 60 months. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_a80cd40ea6db9ff8486b4ad6e10638d2","quote":"Between July 5, 2007, and Nov 15, 2016, 12 546 patients were enrolled and randomly assigned to receive aspirin (n=6270) or placebo (n=6276) at 501 study sites. Median follow-up was 60 months."} -->
- The overall incidence of serious adverse events was similar in both treatment groups (1266 [20.19%] with aspirin vs 1311 [20.89%] with placebo), as was the overall incidence of adverse events (5142 [82.01%] vs 5129 [81.72%]). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_a80cd40ea6db9ff8486b4ad6e10638d2","quote":"The overall incidence rate of serious adverse events was similar in both treatment groups (n=1266 [20·19%] in the aspirin group vs n=1311 [20·89%] in the placebo group. The overall incidence of adverse events was similar in both treatment groups (n=5142 [82·01%] vs n=5129 [81·72%] in the placebo group)."} -->
- There were 321 documented deaths in the intention-to-treat population (160 [2.55%] with aspirin vs 161 [2.57%] with placebo). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_a80cd40ea6db9ff8486b4ad6e10638d2","quote":"There were 321 documented deaths in the intention-to-treat population (n=160 [2·55%] vs n=161 [2·57%] of 6276 patients in the placebo group)."} -->
