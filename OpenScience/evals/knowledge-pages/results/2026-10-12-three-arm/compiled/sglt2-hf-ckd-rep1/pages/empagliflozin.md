# Empagliflozin

## Trials in heart failure

- In EMPEROR-Reduced, 3730 patients with class II, III, or IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of 40% or less were randomly assigned to empagliflozin 10 mg once daily or placebo in addition to recommended therapy. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"we randomly assigned 3730 patients with class II, III, or IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of 40% or less to receive empagliflozin (10 mg once daily) or placebo, in addition to recommended therapy"} -->
- In EMPEROR-Reduced, the total number of hospitalizations for heart failure was lower in the empagliflozin group than in the placebo group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"The total number of hospitalizations for heart failure was lower in the empagliflozin group than in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.70; 95% CI, 0.58 to 0.85; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- In EMPEROR-Reduced, the annual rate of decline in the estimated glomerular filtration rate was slower in the empagliflozin group than in the placebo group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"The annual rate of decline in the estimated glomerular filtration rate was slower in the empagliflozin group than in the placebo group (-0.55 vs. -2.28 ml per minute per 1.73 m2 of body-surface area per year, P\u003c0.001)"} -->
- In EMPEROR-Preserved, 5988 patients with class II-IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of more than 40% were randomly assigned to empagliflozin 10 mg once daily or placebo in addition to usual therapy. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"we randomly assigned 5988 patients with class II-IV heart failure and an ejection fraction of more than 40% to receive empagliflozin (10 mg once daily) or placebo, in addition to usual therapy"} -->
- In EMPEROR-Preserved, the effects of empagliflozin appeared consistent in patients with or without diabetes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"The effects of empagliflozin appeared consistent in patients with or without diabetes."} -->
- In EMPEROR-Preserved, the total number of hospitalizations for heart failure was lower in the empagliflozin group than in the placebo group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"The total number of hospitalizations for heart failure was lower in the empagliflozin group than in the placebo group (407 with empagliflozin and 541 with placebo; hazard ratio, 0.73; 95% CI, 0.61 to 0.88; P\u003c0.001)."} -->

## Trial in chronic kidney disease

- In EMPA-KIDNEY, patients with chronic kidney disease were enrolled if they had an estimated GFR of at least 20 but less than 45 ml per minute per 1.73 m2, or an estimated GFR of at least 45 but less than 90 ml per minute per 1.73 m2 with a urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio of at least 200. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_9babb063478831150659a9e2e6b0e443","quote":"We enrolled patients with chronic kidney disease who had an estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) of at least 20 but less than 45 ml per minute per 1.73 m2 of body-surface area, or who had an eGFR of at least 45 but less than 90 ml per minute per 1.73 m2 with a urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio (with albumin measured in milligrams and creatinine measured in grams) of at least 200."} -->
- In EMPA-KIDNEY, 6609 patients underwent randomization and the rates of serious adverse events were similar in the two groups. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_9babb063478831150659a9e2e6b0e443","quote":"A total of 6609 patients underwent randomization."} --> <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_9babb063478831150659a9e2e6b0e443","quote":"The rates of serious adverse events were similar in the two groups."} -->
