# SGLT2 inhibitors in heart failure

## Effect in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction

- In patients with heart failure and an ejection fraction of 40% or less, dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily reduced the composite of worsening heart failure or cardiovascular death compared with placebo over a median of 18.2 months. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"Over a median of 18.2 months, the primary outcome occurred in 386 of 2373 patients (16.3%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 502 of 2371 patients (21.2%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.74; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.65 to 0.85; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- In patients with heart failure and an ejection fraction of 40% or less, empagliflozin 10 mg once daily reduced the composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for worsening heart failure compared with placebo over a median of 16 months. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"During a median of 16 months, a primary outcome event occurred in 361 of 1863 patients (19.4%) in the empagliflozin group and in 462 of 1867 patients (24.7%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio for cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure, 0.75; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.65 to 0.86; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- The effect of empagliflozin on the primary outcome in heart failure with an ejection fraction of 40% or less was consistent regardless of the presence or absence of diabetes. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"The effect of empagliflozin on the primary outcome was consistent in patients regardless of the presence or absence of diabetes."} -->

## Effect in heart failure with mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction

- In patients with heart failure and an ejection fraction of more than 40%, empagliflozin 10 mg once daily reduced the composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure compared with placebo over a median of 26.2 months. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"Over a median of 26.2 months, a primary outcome event occurred in 415 of 2997 patients (13.8%) in the empagliflozin group and in 511 of 2991 patients (17.1%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.79; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.69 to 0.90; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- In patients with heart failure and an ejection fraction of more than 40%, the effect of empagliflozin was mainly related to a lower risk of hospitalization for heart failure. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"This effect was mainly related to a lower risk of hospitalization for heart failure in the empagliflozin group."} -->
- In patients with heart failure and a left ventricular ejection fraction of more than 40%, dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily reduced the composite of worsening heart failure or cardiovascular death compared with placebo over a median of 2.3 years. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"Over a median of 2.3 years, the primary outcome occurred in 512 of 3131 patients (16.4%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 610 of 3132 patients (19.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.82; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.73 to 0.92; P\u003c0.001)."} -->
- In that trial of dapagliflozin in heart failure with a left ventricular ejection fraction of more than 40%, the incidence of adverse events was similar in the dapagliflozin and placebo groups. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_e1fc1fa13a9a3fbf27de970c81e78bba","quote":"The incidence of adverse events was similar in the two groups."} -->

## Consistency across the ejection fraction spectrum

- Across five randomised trials of 21 947 participants, SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the risk of composite cardiovascular death or hospitalisation for heart failure, cardiovascular death, first hospitalisation for heart failure, and all-cause mortality. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_4b1be72f0031135f81e0915e571e919b","quote":"In the broader context of the five trials of 21 947 participants, SGLT2 inhibitors reduced the risk of composite cardiovascular death or hospitalisation for heart failure (0·77 [0·72-0·82]), cardiovascular death (0·87 [0·79-0·95]), first hospitalisation for heart failure (0·72 [0·67-0·78]), and all-cause mortality (0·92 [0·86-0·99])."} -->
- In the meta-analysis, treatment effects for each studied endpoint were consistently observed both in the trials of heart failure with mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction and across all five trials. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_4b1be72f0031135f81e0915e571e919b","quote":"These treatment effects for each of the studied endpoints were consistently observed in both the trials of heart failure with mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction and across all five trials."} -->
- In the meta-analysis, treatment effects on the primary endpoint were generally consistent across the 14 subgroups examined, including ejection fraction. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_4b1be72f0031135f81e0915e571e919b","quote":"Treatment effects on the primary endpoint were generally consistent across the 14 subgroups examined, including ejection fraction."} -->

## Safety

- In the trial of dapagliflozin in heart failure with a reduced ejection fraction, the frequency of adverse events related to volume depletion, renal dysfunction, and hypoglycemia did not differ between the treatment groups. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_3d8122c20e1cbc078636f036dbe476b7","quote":"The frequency of adverse events related to volume depletion, renal dysfunction, and hypoglycemia did not differ between treatment groups."} -->
- Uncomplicated genital tract infection was reported more frequently with empagliflozin in the trial of patients with heart failure and an ejection fraction of 40% or less. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_b5b2f2860d0e0ed2b6663cb104221791","quote":"Uncomplicated genital tract infection was reported more frequently with empagliflozin."} -->
- In the trial of patients with heart failure and an ejection fraction of more than 40%, uncomplicated genital and urinary tract infections and hypotension were reported more frequently with empagliflozin. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_343c67f35a2bf841907858fd63302653","quote":"Uncomplicated genital and urinary tract infections and hypotension were reported more frequently with empagliflozin."} -->
