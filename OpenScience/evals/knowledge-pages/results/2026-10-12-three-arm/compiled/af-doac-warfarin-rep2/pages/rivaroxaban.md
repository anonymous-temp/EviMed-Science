# Rivaroxaban

## ROCKET AF: rivaroxaban versus warfarin in nonvalvular atrial fibrillation

- In the double-blind ROCKET AF trial, 14,264 patients with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation at increased risk for stroke were randomly assigned to rivaroxaban 20 mg daily or dose-adjusted warfarin, with a per-protocol, as-treated primary analysis designed to determine noninferiority for stroke or systemic embolism. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8ff4bad2e9d926335b4de77ad1e41630","quote":"In a double-blind trial, we randomly assigned 14,264 patients with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation who were at increased risk for stroke to receive either rivaroxaban (at a daily dose of 20 mg) or dose-adjusted warfarin. The per-protocol, as-treated primary analysis was designed to determine whether rivaroxaban was noninferior to warfarin for the primary end point of stroke or systemic embolism."} -->
- Major and nonmajor clinically relevant bleeding occurred in 1475 rivaroxaban patients (14.9% per year) and 1449 warfarin patients (14.5% per year) (hazard ratio 1.03; 95% CI 0.96 to 1.11; P=0.44), with significant reductions in intracranial hemorrhage (0.5% vs. 0.7%, P=0.02) and fatal bleeding (0.2% vs. 0.5%, P=0.003) in the rivaroxaban group. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8ff4bad2e9d926335b4de77ad1e41630","quote":"Major and nonmajor clinically relevant bleeding occurred in 1475 patients in the rivaroxaban group (14.9% per year) and in 1449 in the warfarin group (14.5% per year) (hazard ratio, 1.03; 95% CI, 0.96 to 1.11; P=0.44), with significant reductions in intracranial hemorrhage (0.5% vs. 0.7%, P=0.02) and fatal bleeding (0.2% vs. 0.5%, P=0.003) in the rivaroxaban group."} -->
- In patients with atrial fibrillation, rivaroxaban was noninferior to warfarin for prevention of stroke or systemic embolism, with no significant between-group difference in major bleeding, although intracranial and fatal bleeding occurred less frequently with rivaroxaban. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_8ff4bad2e9d926335b4de77ad1e41630","quote":"In patients with atrial fibrillation, rivaroxaban was noninferior to warfarin for the prevention of stroke or systemic embolism. There was no significant between-group difference in the risk of major bleeding, although intracranial and fatal bleeding occurred less frequently in the rivaroxaban group."} -->

## J-ROCKET AF: a Japan-specific rivaroxaban dose

- J-ROCKET AF was a prospective, randomized, double-blind, phase III trial in which 1,280 Japanese patients with non-valvular atrial fibrillation at increased risk for stroke were randomized to rivaroxaban 15 mg once daily or warfarin dose-adjusted according to Japanese guidelines. <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81808a7c4c3bf0e34bac1c720768f46d","quote":"J-ROCKET AF was a prospective, randomized, double-blind, phase III trial. Patients (n=1,280) with non-valvular AF at increased risk for stroke were randomized to receive 15 mg once-daily rivaroxaban or warfarin dose-adjusted according to Japanese guidelines."} -->
- Noninferiority of rivaroxaban to warfarin was confirmed for the principal safety outcome of major and non-major clinically relevant bleeding, at 18.04% per year with rivaroxaban and 16.42% per year with warfarin (hazard ratio 1.11; 95% CI 0.87-1.42; P<0.001 for noninferiority). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81808a7c4c3bf0e34bac1c720768f46d","quote":"Non-inferiority of rivaroxaban to warfarin was confirmed; the rate of the principal safety outcome was 18.04% per year in rivaroxaban-treated patients and 16.42% per year in warfarin-treated patients (hazard ratio [HR] 1.11; 95% confidence interval 0.87-1.42; P\u003c0.001 [non-inferiority])."} -->
- Intracranial hemorrhage rates were 0.8% with rivaroxaban and 1.6% with warfarin, and there was a strong trend for a reduction in stroke or systemic embolism with rivaroxaban versus warfarin (hazard ratio 0.49; P=0.050). <!-- evimed-source:{"sourceId":"src_81808a7c4c3bf0e34bac1c720768f46d","quote":"Intracranial hemorrhage rates were 0.8% with rivaroxaban and 1.6% with warfarin.
